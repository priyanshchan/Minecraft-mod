# Overlord: Tomb of Nazarick — Minecraft Bedrock add-on

A fan-made add-on for **Minecraft Bedrock Edition** inspired by the anime *Overlord*.
It adds Ainz Ooal Gown and the Floor Guardians as bosses, Nazarick undead that roam the
night, a rideable Hamsuke, legendary weapons with spells, an armour set, blocks and a
complete survival progression.

- **Ready-to-install file:** [`dist/Overlord_Tomb_of_Nazarick.mcaddon`](dist/Overlord_Tomb_of_Nazarick.mcaddon), also attached to each [release](../../releases)
- **Requires:** Minecraft Bedrock **1.21.90 or newer** (vanilla IDs checked against the 1.26.60 game data)
- **No experimental toggles needed** — only stable features and the stable `@minecraft/server` / `@minecraft/server-ui` 2.0.0 script APIs are used
- Every texture, icon and model is original pixel art generated by the scripts in `tools/`

## Install

1. Download `Overlord_Tomb_of_Nazarick.mcaddon` from the `dist` folder.
2. Open the file with Minecraft (tap it on Android/iOS, double-click on Windows). Both packs import automatically.
3. Create or edit a world, then under **Behavior Packs** activate *Overlord: Tomb of Nazarick [BP]*.
   The resource pack is attached automatically.
4. Play. All items are in the creative inventory; the survival route is described below.

Consoles cannot open `.mcaddon` files directly; use a Realm or a world prepared on another device.

### How the creatures look and move

- **True to size.** A grown man stands exactly as tall as the player; women, youths, goblins and dwarves are
  smaller; and the big things are big — an Ogre is half again a man's height, a War Troll nearly twice, a
  Frost Giant well over twice, a Primal Earth Elemental and the Dark Young about three times, and the dragons
  dwarf them all (the Platinum Dragon Lord is the largest creature in the add-on). Hit boxes match.
- **More detail.** Every creature's texture is drawn at twice the resolution with light falling from above,
  bevelled edges, a thin shadow under each painted feature and a little grain. The shared body types gained
  geometry as well: hair that stands off the head, pauldrons on armoured soldiers, capes on knights, captains
  and priests, belt pouches, tusks, ears and loincloths on ogres, trolls and giants, spines, ears and tails
  on the four-legged beasts, back ridges and bracers on lizardmen and beastmen, and extra horns, flank
  plates and a spiked tail on the dragons.
- **Alive.** Every creature breathes, recoils when struck and leans into its stride; a fighter's off arm and
  head follow each swing; capes stir at rest and stream out behind a runner; beasts' tails sway.
- **Presence.** Something hangs in the air around the notable ones — shadow round Ainz, Death Knights and
  the undead lords, embers round demons, drifting frost round Cocytus and the ice dragons, shafts of light
  round angels, crackling arcs round Narberal and the Platinum Dragon Lord, runes round the great casters.
  A melee blow leaves a crescent in the attacker's colour, deaths are marked with a burst (a skull over the
  undead, a nova for a great foe), and any great foe arrives inside a magic circle.

### The great dragons

The Frost Dragon, the Skeletal Dragon and the Platinum Dragon Lord are the largest creatures in the add-on
and each has its own model on a shared skeleton — a long neck in two parts, a hinged jaw, four clawed legs, a
four-part tail and two-part wings.

| Dragon | Length | Wingspan | Look | Breath |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Frost Dragon** | about 23 blocks | about 20 | Pale blue scales, tall ice spines, a beard of icicles, a mace of ice on its tail | *Frost Breath*: a blast of cold (14 damage, Slowness III) that leaves the ground freezing for a moment |
| **Skeletal Dragon** | about 24 blocks | about 22 | An open ribcage round a green soul fire, a bare skull, tattered wings | *Breath of Undeath*: negative energy (12 damage, Wither) |
| **Platinum Dragon Lord** | about 30 blocks | about 27 | Plated flanks, a crown of golden horns, a glowing gem in its chest, a bladed tail | *Judgment*: five bolts of lightning, one on the mark and four around it (10 damage each) |

- **Animation:** wings beat in two parts, harder when the dragon is moving; the neck weaves; the jaw gapes to
  bite and now and then to snarl; a wave runs down the tail; a bite is a lunge of the whole neck; and like
  every creature they breathe and recoil when struck.
- **Roar:** a dragon roars at the first player it sees, with rings rolling out from its feet and the view
  shaking for everyone within 48 blocks.
- **Presence:** frost drifts from the Frost Dragon's wings and tail, ice falls from them and mist rolls off
  its flanks; soul fire burns in the Skeletal Dragon's ribs, shadow curls round it and its eye sockets glow;
  lightning crawls along the Platinum Dragon Lord's wings, light rises beneath it and runes circle its crown.
- **Footfalls:** the ground shudders and dust rises as a dragon walks.
- **Breath** (every 9 seconds, up to 34 blocks): a circle marks where it will land while light gathers in the
  dragon's jaws; about a second later the breath strikes that spot. Step off the mark and it misses.
- **Wing buffet** (every 14 seconds, when you are close): the wings draw back, then a gale hurts and throws
  back everyone near.
- **Enrage:** at half health a dragon roars again in a pillar of its element, gains Speed and Strength, and
  breathes and buffets a third faster.
- **Death:** its element tears loose along its whole length — pillars at the head, wings, tail and chest in
  turn, then a dome of magic circles.

- **Flight (v7.5.5):** once a dragon has lost a sixth of its health — and then every half minute or so, every
  20 seconds once enraged — it **takes to the sky** with a gust that throws back anyone beside it. It climbs
  about 15 blocks, **circles** high over its foe (following if you run) and **rains breath** down every 3
  seconds (2 when enraged). Then it announces a **dive**, hangs over the spot it has chosen — a glowing circle
  on the ground — and plunges onto it: everyone within 6.5 blocks of the landing takes a heavy blow and is
  thrown back. Aloft its legs fold, its neck reaches forward and its wings beat hard; only arrows and spells
  can reach it until it lands.

Hit boxes are the dragons' trunks (about 4 to 5 blocks wide and 5 to 7 tall), so they can walk through open
country; they also step over ledges two blocks high.

### Weapons and armour (v7.2)

Every weapon of the add-on now belongs to a **kind** and an **element**, and fights in its style:

- **Crescents:** each melee blow leaves a crescent of the weapon's colour on the target.
- **Combos:** land three blows within about 1.5 seconds of each other and the third is your weapon kind's **finisher** (the action bar counts the chain).
- **Awakening:** the harm you deal fills a gauge. When it is full your weapon sparks in your hand; **sneak + use** it to awaken it for 12 seconds — every blow hits 35% harder and bursts in its element on everything around the target, and the weapon blazes in your hand.
- **Weapon arts:** using a weapon (right-click / hold) unleashes its art. Eleven weapons that had none now have one.
- **Animations:** five new attack poses — a full spin, a thrust, an uppercut, an iaido draw and the two-handed awakening — alongside the slash and overhead slam.
- **Look:** weapon and armour icons are now 32×32: lit from the top right, with bright cutting edges, glinting gems and a faint glow of the weapon's element. Armour as worn is 128×64 with bevelled, shaded plates.
- The **Arsenal** entry of the Adventurer's Plate shows the weapon in your hand (kind, art, finisher, gauge) and the armour you wear (pieces, bonuses, art).

### Boss fights (v7.5)

Every boss of the add-on — the Floor Guardians and Ainz, Pandora's Actor, Gargantua and Rubedo, the great
demons and angels, the world bosses and the arena champions — now fights in **three phases**. (The three
dragons keep their own staged fights.)

- **Telegraphed moves:** before each move the boss's name and the move appear on your screen and glowing
  circles or lines mark where it will land. A second or two later it lands on whoever is still standing there,
  so **move out**. Shapes: a **slam** round the boss, a **ring** that is safe close in, **lines** (single,
  fanned or crossed) towards a target, a **barrage** under every player, a **pull** that drags you in before a
  slam, and a **blink** that puts the boss behind its target.
- **Phases:** at two-thirds and one-third of its health the boss changes phase — a title on screen, a
  moment's resistance, a blast that throws back anyone close, allies for some bosses, and harder moves. The
  last phase is **enraged**: Strength, Speed and a burning aura, with moves coming faster (every 7 s, then 5.5 s,
  then 4 s).
- **Hazards:** some moves leave burning, frozen or poisoned ground that hurts anyone standing on it for a few
  seconds.
- **Rewards:** everyone who fought a boss gets **2–4 Refining Stones** and **a gem** (two from bosses with
  700+ health), and the **first time** you defeat each boss, a **Codex of the Supreme Beings**.

### RPG system

Every player gets an **Adventurer's Plate** on first join (recipe: paper + copper ingot). Use it to open the
character menu.

- **Levels (1–100):** defeating any mob gives XP; bosses give far more. Each level grants 3 stat points and a
  skill point (see *Skill tree* below).
  Your level is shown under your name for other players.
- **Stats (up to 50 each):** Strength (+2% melee damage per point), Vitality (+2 hearts per 5 points),
  Intelligence (+2% spell power and +5 mana per point), Agility (Speed I/II/III at 10/25/45 points).
- **Mana:** staff, ring, tome and weapon abilities cost mana, which recovers over time. Scrolls cost none.
  **Mana Potions** restore 80 (recipe: lapis, data crystal, lapis over 3 glass bottles → 3; both merchants sell them).
- **Classes** (change any time): Warrior (+20% melee, +2 hearts, 30% less mana), Magic Caster (+30% spell power,
  +50% mana, faster recovery), Assassin (25% critical hits for +50% damage, extra Speed), Cleric (slow health
  regeneration, +30% melee damage to undead).
- **Class skills:** sneak and use the plate to trigger your class's active skill (45 s cooldown) — Fortress
  (Warrior: Resistance II), Mana Surge (Magic Caster: refills mana), Shadow Step (Assassin: invisible and
  fast), Sanctuary (Cleric: heals you and nearby players).
- **Class mastery:** at level 40 your class takes its advanced form and its skill recharges in 30 s —
  Sword Saint (+30% melee, +4 hearts, Fortress adds Strength), High Wizard (+45% spell power, Mana Surge adds
  Regeneration), Master Assassin (35% critical hits, 10-second Shadow Step), High Priest (double regeneration,
  +45% against undead, Sanctuary reaches 12 blocks and heals your companions).
- **Guild quests:** 56 requests, one at a time, paying Gold Coins and XP. Most are kill requests; seven ask for
  deeds instead — discovering sites, clearing hostile camps, finishing daily bounties, winning a Tomb Raid,
  winning in the arena, slaying elites and defeating a world boss. Completed quests raise your adventurer rank — Copper, Iron,
  Silver, Gold, Platinum, Mithril, Orichalcum, Adamantite — which unlocks harder requests, up to defeating
  Ainz Ooal Gown.
- **Mana font:** standing on a Block of Data Crystal triples your mana recovery.
- **Party play:** players within 24 blocks of a kill receive half its XP and quest credit.
- **Status bar:** level, class, mana, XP and quest progress above the hotbar (can be hidden from the menu).

Progress is saved per player in the world.

### Skill tree

Open **Skill Tree** from the Adventurer's Plate (the first entry after the built-in ones). It sits on top of
stats and classes, which work as before.

- **Skill points:** one at every level from level 2 and one more at every tenth level (109 at level 100),
  plus one for each **Codex of the Supreme Beings** you read, up to ten. A Codex drops from world bosses, the
  Frost Dragon, the Skeletal Dragon, the Platinum Dragon Lord (two) and Tomb Raids of tier 3 (one) and tier 4
  (two). The status bar shows unspent points as `+N SP`.
- **Six paths, 66 skills, about 190 ranks:** far more than any one character can learn, so you have to
  choose. Each path has five tiers; a tier opens once you have spent enough points in that path *and*
  reached its level — tier I: any time, II: 3 points and level 10, III: 8 points and level 20, IV: 14 points
  and level 35, V: 20 points and level 50. Some skills also need an earlier skill of their path.
- **Capstones:** the fifth tier of every path holds one great skill — Six Light Strikes, Maximize Magic,
  Assassinate, Halo of the Six Gods, Authority of the Supreme Being and Midas Touch. Mastering one is
  announced to the whole server.
- **The menu** shows every path with a progress bar, every skill with its rank and why it is locked, and for
  each skill what it does now, at its next rank and at full rank. *Your bonuses* lists everything you have
  learned on one page.
- **Unlearning:** forget everything and spend your points again for 10 Gold Coins plus 2 per point spent.
- Skills feed the systems they belong to: melee damage and critical hits, spell power, mana, cooldowns and
  cost, companions' damage, health and attack rate, how many creatures you can summon, travel and forge
  prices, camp and bounty rewards, XP, and the death penalty.

- **Bond:** every kill at your side deepens your bond with a member (elites count three). Each of the four
  ranks — Companion, Trusted, Loyal, Sworn — adds 8% skill power and takes 10 seconds off the time a fallen
  member is away. A daily gift (5 Gold Coins) adds 5, and a Sworn companion gives you 5 Data Crystals.
- **Traits:** a member can hold one trait from level 10 and two from level 30, changed freely: Sturdy
  (+4 hearts), Keen (skills +15%), Swift (skills recharge 15% faster), Devoted (bond grows twice as fast),
  Scavenger (one kill in ten turns up a Gold Coin), Resolute (returns in half the time).
- **Formations:** three or more members of different vocations recharge skills a fifth faster; members who
  share a vocation strike 15% harder.
- **Looking after themselves:** members recover health between fights; a badly hurt member drinks one of your
  Minor Healing Potions (can be switched off) and, unless it is a Fighter, falls back from monsters until it
  has recovered.
- **Gear:** five ranks bought with Gold Coins (20, 40, 60, 80, 100); each adds 4 hearts, every second one adds
  damage.
- **Knowledge:** members call out what they know the first time you strike a kind of foe, point out elites,
  warn you at nightfall and when you are badly hurt, and an Archer reports the nearest undiscovered site
  every few minutes.
- **Status:** hold the Command Banner to see every member's health above the hotbar.
- **Falling:** a fallen member returns after 60 seconds (less with a strong bond, half with a Healer still
  standing or the Resolute trait), or at once for 8 Gold Coins. Players cannot hurt retainers, their kills
  count as yours for XP, quests and the bestiary, and feeding one bread or a Healing Potion heals it.

### Bestiary and reputation

- **Bestiary:** every kind of foe you (or your party) slay is recorded. At 5, 15 and 40 kills you gain a
  knowledge rank worth +5% damage against it, and rank 1 reveals the veteran's tip for that foe.
- **Reputation:** the Kingdom, the Empire, the Adventurers' Guild and the Lizardman Tribes each track their
  opinion of you (−100 to 100). Killing their people costs 15; at −30 their troops attack you on sight.
  Grudges fade by 1 a minute. Guild quests (+3), slain bandits (+1) and cleared camps (+5) raise it.
- **Clearing camps:** defeat every defender of a hostile site (a Goblin Camp, Bandit Camp, Nazarick Outpost,
  Troll Den, Ruined Keep, Mage Tower, Besieged Mine, Dragon's Hoard and the like) to clear it for
  Gold Coins and XP (more at higher levels). A cleared camp stays quiet for twenty minutes instead of ten.

### Daily bounty, elites and titles

- **Daily bounty:** every in-game day the guild posts one bounty for all players (for example 10 Goblins or
  4 Ogres). Completing it pays Gold Coins and XP that grows with your level. Finishing bounties on consecutive
  days builds a streak worth +10% a day, up to +50%; missing a day resets it.
- **Elite mobs:** about one levelled monster in twenty spawns as an elite — five levels higher, with 10 extra
  hearts, more damage and Resistance. Elites are marked in the target readout, give triple XP and drop Gold
  Coins and a Data Crystal.
- **Titles:** seventeen titles are earned through deeds (Goblin Slayer, Undead Bane, Demon Hunter, World's Shield,
  Elite Hunter, Bounty Hunter, Commander, Sworn Lord, Archmage, Grandmaster, Martial Lord, Giant Killer, Dragon Slayer,
  Demon Emperor's Bane, Guardian's Bane, Adamantite Adventurer, Rival of the Sorcerer King). Each pays 10 Gold Coins when earned, and one can be worn in front of your name.

All three are reached from the Adventurer's Plate menu.

### Runes, the arena and World Items

- **Runes:** stand near a Dwarf Runesmith with a weapon in your hand and a rune in your inventory, and choose
  *Forge* in the plate menu. A weapon bears one rune at a time, written on the item beside any reinforcement;
  a new rune replaces the old one. Runes work on every melee blow:

  | Rune | Effect |
  | --- | --- |
  | Fire | Sets the target alight |
  | Frost | Slows the target |
  | Storm | A second jolt of 4 lightning damage a moment after each blow |
  | Life | Heals you for 8% of the damage dealt |

- **Imperial Arena:** speak to the Arena Master at an Imperial Arena to fight five rounds on the sand. The
  next champions come only when the sand is clear; falling, walking more than 32 blocks away or taking longer
  than two and a half minutes over a round forfeits. The tier follows your level (1–19, 20–49, 50+) and pays
  20, 45 or 90 Gold Coins and 500, 1800 or 5000 XP. At the top tier the fourth round is Zero and the last is
  Go Gin, the Martial Lord; felling him also earns a Guardian Crest and, the first time, a World Item.
- **World Items:** three relics for the end of the game — Avarice and Generosity, the Depiction of Nature and
  Society and Longinus (see the item table).

### World bosses, reinforcing and travel

- **World bosses:** once an in-game day there is a 30% chance that a boss appears 36 blocks from a random
  overworld player of level 25 or higher, and every player is told who it is and in which direction. Below
  level 50 it is Gargantua, Dominion Authority, Evil Lord Wrath, the Skeletal Dragon or a Primal Earth
  Elemental; from level 50 the Frost Dragon, Evil Lord Wrath, Zesshi, the Dark Young, Jaldabaoth or Decem
  Hougan; from level 75 a Floor Guardian (Cocytus, Shalltear, Albedo, Demiurge or Sebas), the Dark Young,
  Jaldabaoth or the Platinum Dragon Lord. Every player within 64 blocks when it dies receives 30 Gold Coins, 6 Data Crystals and
  1500 XP on top of its normal drops.
- **Reinforcing weapons:** stand within 10 blocks of a Dwarf Runesmith, hold one of the add-on's weapons and
  choose *Reinforce weapon* in the plate menu. Each level adds 6% melee damage, up to +5, and is written on
  the item. Level *n* costs 15 × *n* Gold Coins and *n* Adamantite Ingots (75 coins and 5 ingots for +5).
- **Travel:** friendly sites you have visited (Adventurers' Camps, Kingdom Army Camps, Empire Outposts,
  Dwarven Forges, Lizardman Villages, Carne Village, Roadside Inns, Imperial Arenas, Holy Kingdom Forts and
  Elven Groves) are
  remembered — the latest 24. The *Travel* entry in the plate menu
  takes you back to one for 5 Gold Coins, within the overworld.

### Living world

- **Mob levels:** monsters that spawn near you take a level close to yours (70–120% of the highest-level player
  within 64 blocks). Every 10 levels adds 2 hearts, levels 30/60/90 add Strength I/II/III, and each level adds
  3% XP. The level shows next to the mob's name when you hit it. Bosses keep their own numbers.
- **Level-gated spawns:** dangerous mobs only appear once someone nearby is strong enough — Skeleton Mage 3,
  Ogre 4, Elder Lich 5, Beastman 6, Death Knight 8, Shadow Demon 8, Clementine 10, Scripture Priest 10,
  Bafolk 10, Hellhound 12, Soul Eater 12, Frost Giant 14, Gigant Basilisk 15, Fluder 15, Eight-Edge
  Assassin 25. Raids, patrols, summons and
  creative-mode spawning are not affected.
- **Night patrols:** from level 5, each night has a 35% chance that Nazarick sends a patrol towards you. Its
  make-up follows your level (5+, 20+, 50+).
- **Factions:** monsters and Nazarick's rank and file attack human NPCs as well as players; adventurers,
  guards, Gazef, Brain, Evileye and the lizardmen hunt monsters.
- **Behaviour:** goblins, Old Guarders, Quagoa, lizardmen, adventurers and guards call their pack when one is
  hit; goblins flee when badly hurt; Elder Liches, Skeleton Mages and Fluder back away when you close in;
  Soul Eaters leap; Old Guarders and Skeleton Mages burn in daylight; a Death Knight's shield halves
  projectile damage and its human victims rise as zombies.
- **Biomes:** goblins keep to forests, plains and taiga; ogres to plains, forests, hills and savanna; lizardmen
  to swamps; Quagoa underground.
- **Dialogue:** talk to adventurers, guards, Gazef, Climb, Brain, Zaryusu, Evileye or Eclair for a line of advice.

sometimes tell you the same.

#### God-tier magic (v7.6)

Three spells beyond super-tier, meant to be overkill. Each costs 180–200 mana, recharges for five minutes, and
is staged over many seconds:
- The caster kneels and rises with arms to the sky in a 3-second invocation.
- Three magic circles spread from their feet, with a pillar of light and a dome of runes.
- The camera pulls back to watch the whole spell.
- Everyone within 72 blocks sees the spell's name in great letters, and the whole server is told who
  invoked it.

They strike only monsters: villagers, animals, companions and the friendly folk of the world are spared.

| Spell | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **The Goal of All Life Is Death** (dark) | Dread slows and weakens every monster within 40 blocks. A clock of twelve marks forms round you, ten blocks across; a mark lights every half second as a hand of shadow sweeps round, with a bell tolling lower each time. When it strikes, the world goes black for a moment. Every lesser monster within 40 blocks **dies**, and bosses lose a third of their health. Skulls rise from the dead, their souls stream into you (2 health and 5 mana each), and four **Death Knights** rise round you to serve for a minute. |

Summoned creatures follow you and attack monsters. You can command four at a time between summoning spells
and Sealing Crystals; Death Knights from the Tome: Create Undead are counted separately.

**Scrolls and tomes** are tied to tier magic: using a scroll or tome of a spell you know (Call Greater
Thunder, Fly, Time Stop, Heavy Recover → Heal, Greater Teleportation → Dimensional Move, Reality Slash, Fallen
Down) counts as a cast towards its mastery, the Orb of Death counts for Summon Undead 3rd Tier, and reading
the Tome: Reality Slash or Tome: Fallen Down teaches you that spell for free once your level allows its tier.

Spell names and tiers follow the Overlord fan wiki's Tier Spells and character pages where it lists them;
the in-game effects are this add-on's own interpretation.

### Spell visuals

Every spell, scroll, tome and weapon skill has its own effects, drawn with 32 custom particle effects that
are tinted by element (purple arcane, orange fire, pale-blue ice, yellow lightning, gold holy, green acid,
violet dark, white for the unaligned) and rendered additively so they glow.

- **Magic circles:** an outer ring of runes and an inner hexagram turning against it under the caster,
  larger at higher tiers; runes drift up from the big ones, and super-tier magic stacks them into a dome.
- **Gathering power:** light flares in the casting hand, and from the sixth tier sparkles rush inwards as
  the spell builds. Delayed spells pulse their target circle while power gathers overhead.
- **Beams:** a row of motes around one bright core laid from hand to target, with a flash where it leaves
  the caster.
- **Impacts:** sparkles and a flash, then the element's own signature —

  | Element | On impact | Along a beam | Rising from the ground |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | Fire | Licking flames and embers | Embers | Embers |
  | Ice | Tumbling shards | Shards | — |
  | Lightning | Jagged arcs that flicker for an instant | Arcs every two blocks | — |
  | Holy | Shafts of light | — | Shafts of light |
  | Dark | Curling wisps of shadow | Wisps | Wisps |
  | Acid | A spatter of droplets | Droplets | — |
  | Arcane | Drifting runes | — | Runes |
  | Unaligned | Streaks of light | — | — |

- **Area spells:** a pillar of light, a nova of streaks, and a shock wave that rolls out along the ground
  with dust behind it. The largest shake the view of everyone nearby.
- **Buffs and heals:** a second of sparkles climbing around whoever is affected.
- **Death magic:** a skull rises over anything a dark spell kills, and over the victim of Grasp Heart.
- **Teleporting:** you vanish in a rush of gathered light and arrive in a shaft of it; your view darkens for
  a blink. Time Stop blanches it.
- **Scrolls** are held open and read — runes lift off the page and it burns away in the colour of its
  spell — and each has its own effect (a thunderbolt with a nova, a whirl of wind for Fly, a fireball's
  blast for Explosion, and so on). **Tomes** are held open in the off hand while the other traces the words.
- **Weapon skills** are swings: a crescent in the air for cuts, a ground ring for slams.
- **Twelve poses** on the player model, each with a wind-up, a strike and a recovery: hand thrust, arms
  raised, arms spread then drawn in, sweep, slam, channelling, summoning, the long super-tier cast, reading
  a scroll, reading a tome, weapon slash and weapon slam. They are visible in third person and to other
  players.

- **Light.** Spells light up their surroundings: the casting hand, the ground under a magic circle, the
  length of a beam, the point of impact, a pillar from base to top and a nova out to its edge. It is real
  block light (an invisible light block set for a fraction of a second and then removed, only ever in air),
  so it shows in every graphics mode and, with Vibrant Visuals, falls on the scene the way a torch's does.
  The light is white rather than coloured. **Grimoire → Spell lighting** switches it off for the world.
- **What a spell leaves behind.** Flames burn on and the ground is scorched where fire struck; mist hangs
  where cold, shadow or acid struck; lightning goes on crackling over a scorch mark; light and runes keep
  rising after holy and arcane magic. Beams have a soft glow round their core, and motes circle a caster of
  the fifth tier and above.

When a great deal is drawn in the same instant the decorative layers are dropped first, so a big fight keeps
its basic effects without flooding the screen. **Grimoire → Preview the eight elements** plays each
element's effects around you, which is the quickest way to see them all.

### Combat mechanics

- Hitting a mob shows its name and remaining health above the hotbar.
- Agility gives a 0.4% critical-hit chance per point (+50% damage); Assassins add 25% on top.
- Dying costs a tenth of the XP gathered towards your next level (never a level).

### Tomb Raid

Blow the **Horn of the Tomb Raid** to start a five-wave battle around you. The tier follows your level
(1–19, 20–49, 50–79, 80+): tier 1 ends with Death Knights, tier 2 with a random Pleiades maid, tier 3 with a
random Floor Guardian, and tier 4 — the Throne Room — sends Pleiades and Cocytus before Ainz Ooal Gown
himself. Clear every wave to earn Gold Coins, Data Crystals and XP (24/6/600, 48/12/2000, 96/24/6000 or
160/40/12000, with Guardian Crests and Soul Fragments at the higher tiers). The raid fails if you die, move more than
48 blocks away or take longer than four minutes on a wave.

## Repository layout

```
behavior_pack/   entities, items, blocks, recipes, loot tables, spawn rules, trading, scripts/
resource_pack/   models, textures, animations, particles, attachables, sounds, texts
dist/            packaged .mcaddon
tools/gen/       generator for models, textures and JSON (Python + Pillow + NumPy)
tools/validate.py  cross-reference checker
tools/build.py     validates, then packages dist/*.mcaddon
tools/test_rpg.py  runs the script logic against a mock of the game API (needs Node.js)
docs/releases/     release notes, one file per version
.github/workflows/release.yml  publishes a GitHub release with the .mcaddon attached (run it from the Actions tab)
```

`behavior_pack/scripts/main.js` (spells and item abilities) and `rpg.js` (levels, mana, classes, quests), `spells.js` (tier magic), `vfx.js` (spell visuals), `raid.js` (Tomb Raid), `director.js` (mob levels, gated
spawns, patrols, guards, reputation), `structures.js` (camps and outposts), `party.js` (retainers), `guild.js` (daily bounty, titles), `forge.js` (weapon reinforcement), `mount.js` (Hamsuke), `arena.js` (Imperial Arena), `skills.js` (skill tree data and rules), `skilltree.js` (skill tree menus and effects) `arms.js` (weapon kinds, combos, awakening and arts; armour bonuses, auras and arts), `bosses.js` (boss phases, telegraphed moves, hazards and rewards), `gear.js` (refining, gems and salvage) and `dungeon.js` (the Great Tomb) are
written by hand; everything else inside the two pack
folders is produced by `tools/gen/generate.py`, so change the generator rather than the output.

```bash
python3 tools/gen/generate.py   # regenerate packs
python3 tools/validate.py       # check every icon, texture, model, sound and id reference
python3 tools/build.py          # validate + write dist/Overlord_Tomb_of_Nazarick.mcaddon
```
