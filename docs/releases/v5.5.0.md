## Spell, scroll and weapon visuals rebuilt

This release is about how magic looks. Nothing about damage, costs or cooldowns has changed.

- **25 custom particle effects** (there were 5), drawn additively so they glow, from 14 new textures.
- **Layered magic circles:** an outer ring of runes with an inner hexagram turning against it; runes rise
  from the larger ones.
- **A signature for each element** on impact, along beams and rising from the ground: flames and embers,
  ice shards, lightning arcs, shafts of holy light, wisps of shadow, acid droplets, drifting runes.
- **Beams** have a bright core and a flash at the caster's hand; **area spells** add a nova, a pillar of
  light and a shock wave with dust, and the largest shake the view.
- **Gathering power:** light in the casting hand, sparkles rushing inwards from the sixth tier, and target
  circles that pulse while delayed spells charge.
- **Scrolls** are read and burn away in the colour of their spell, each with its own effect; **tomes** are
  held open; **weapon skills** are drawn as swings.
- **Death magic** leaves a skull over what it kills. Teleporting and Time Stop briefly wash the view.
- **Twelve casting and weapon poses** (there were three), each with a wind-up, a strike and a recovery.
- **Grimoire → Preview the eight elements** plays every element's effects around you.

When a lot is drawn at once the decorative layers are dropped first.

## Install

Download `Overlord_Tomb_of_Nazarick.mcaddon` below and open it with Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.90 or newer, then
enable both packs on your world. No experimental toggles are needed.

The effect definitions pass the automated checks, but they have not been seen in the game itself. If an
effect looks wrong or does not show, please open an issue saying which spell it was.
