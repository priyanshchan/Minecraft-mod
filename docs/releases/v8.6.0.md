## The Dead and the Deep Forest

A content drop: new creatures, a world boss, weapons, an armour set, spells and places to find them.

### New creatures
- **Ghouls:** they hunt in packs at night, leap at you, and their bite slows you.
- **Wraiths:** they vanish and step out of the air **behind you**, and their touch withers.
- **Death Cavaliers:** undead knights of the plains that charge with their lances.
- **Treants:** slow walking trees of the forest whose roots hold you fast.
- **Pinison Pole Perlia:** the dryad of the Dryad's Spring, who trades saplings, golden apples, potions and moss.
- **Zy'tl Q'ae, the Destroyer Tree:** a new world boss with 1300 health and three phases.
  - Its moves: Root Eruption, Vine Lash, Seed Barrage, Uprooting Grasp, Fall of the World Tree, and Saplings
    Awaken, which calls up Treants.
  - It sleeps in the Grove of the Destroyer Tree and wakes for players of level 50 or more.

### New weapons, each 3D in the hand with its own art
- **Lance of the Death Cavalier:** Death Charge rides down a 9-block line, withering it.
- **Ghoulfang Sickle:** Paralysing Rend makes three quick cuts and leaves the foe barely able to move.
- **Heartwood Staff:** Grasping Roots bursts roots from the ground where you look, binding and crushing every
  foe near.

### New armour: Death Cavalier
- Black plate with a spectral green glow and a cape, worn in 3D.
- Two pieces: Speed I.
- Full set: Strength I and Resistance I.
- Double-tap sneak for **Spectral Charge**.

### New spells
- **Paralysis** (tier 4)
- **Summon Undead 5th Tier** (tier 5), which summons two Ghouls
- **Wail of the Banshee** (tier 7)

### New places
- **Grove of the Destroyer Tree:** a ring of dead black trees.
- **Katze Plains Barrow:** a haunted burial mound.
- **Dryad's Spring:** a friendly pool you can travel back to.

### New materials
Ghoul Fangs, Spectral Iron and Heartwood of the Destroyer.

## Install

Download `Overlord_Tomb_of_Nazarick.mcaddon` below and open it with Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.90 or newer, then
enable both packs on your world. No experimental toggles are needed. Saves from older versions keep working.
The new places appear in parts of the world you haven't explored yet.

The new content was checked in a simulation of the game's script API. The creatures, weapons, armour and
places were drawn offline. None of it has been played in Minecraft itself yet.
