## v9.6.0: The Siege of the Great Wall and Familiars

A massive content drop: a **third dungeon** in the burning Holy Kingdom, and **familiars**, little companions
that follow you, level up and learn new abilities.

### The Siege of the Great Wall: a third dungeon
Jaldabaoth's demons and the demi-human tribes have broken the Great Wall of the Holy Kingdom.

- **Getting in:** stand at the **heart of the ritual circle** of a **Demon Gate** (newly generated gates), or use
  a **Seal of the Great Wall**. Seals are found in Demon Gate and Holy Kingdom Fort chests, or crafted from a
  Demon's Heart, Angel Feathers and gold. Use the Seal inside to climb back out.
- **The Breached Wall:** bafolk and beastmen, then **Buser, King of the Bafolk** (new boss). Watch for **burning
  pitch** traps.
- **The Burning City:** hellhounds and shadow demons, then **two new bosses at once**: **Evil Lord Envy** and
  **Evil Lord Greed**. Watch for **hellfire brands** in the ground.
- **Jaldabaoth's Pyre:** a huge hall. Evil Lord Wrath and his hounds, then **Jaldabaoth**, the Demon Emperor.
- **Rewards:** treasure on every floor; breaking the siege pays 180 gold, Demon's Hearts, Data Crystals, Angel
  Feathers and a ruby.
- **The Demonbane Halberd of the Holy Kingdom** (new 3D polearm, damage 14): given the first time you break
  the siege (and sometimes after).
  - Demons take a third more from every blow.
  - Its art, **Judgement of the Great Wall** (sneak + use), raises a wall of holy light along the ground ahead,
    burning everything in it and hurling demons back.
- **New title:** Saviour of the Holy Kingdom.

### Familiars
Four little companions, bound by using their token:

| Familiar | Token (where to get it) | Level 1 | Level 4 | Level 8 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Frost Dragonling** | Frost Dragonling Egg (Frost Dragons, or 8 Frost Dragon Scales + an egg) | Frost Breath | Frost Ward | Winter Gale |
| **Hanzo** | Hanzo's Contract (Eight-Edge Assassins, or written) | Venom Fang | Pickpocket | Shadow Mark |
| **Shadow Demon** | Shadow Demon's Contract (Shadow Demons, or written with a Demon's Heart) | Darkvision | Scout | Shadow Bind |
| **Cherub** | Cherub's Halo (Archangel Flames, or made of feathers and gold) | Mending | Purify | Guardian Light |

- Your first familiar comes with a **Familiar's Bell**. Use it to call your familiar or send it home; sneak +
  use (or the **Familiars** page on the Adventurer's Plate) to choose which one, rename it and see its tricks.
- One familiar is out at a time. It **can't be hurt**, follows you, and catches up if left behind.
- It **levels up** (to 10) from the foes you defeat while it's out, and learns a new trick at levels 1, 4 and 8:
  - **Frost Dragonling:** breathes frost at foes; wraps you in ice when you're badly hurt; a winter gale that
    throws back everything around you.
  - **Hanzo:** poisons foes; steals extra gold from what you defeat; marks the strongest foe, weakening it.
  - **Shadow Demon:** lets you see in the dark; tells you where undiscovered places are; binds whatever hits you.
  - **Cherub:** slowly heals you; lifts poison, wither, weakness and slowness; a burst of healing light when
    you're close to death.
- **New titles:** Beastmaster of Nazarick (all four familiars) and Soul-Bonded (a familiar at level 10).

### Behind the scenes
The Dwarven Royal Capital and the Great Wall now run on one shared dungeon engine, and no dungeon is ever
built on top of another.

## Install

Download `Overlord_Tomb_of_Nazarick.mcaddon` below and open it with Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.90 or newer, then
enable both packs on your world. No experimental toggles are needed. Saves from older versions keep working.

The Great Wall, the familiars and the Halberd were checked in a simulation of the game's script API, and the
halls, new bosses, familiars and Halberd were drawn offline. None of it has been played in Minecraft itself yet.
