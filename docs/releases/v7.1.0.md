## The skill tree

Open **Skill Tree** from your Adventurer's Plate. It adds to your stats and class rather than replacing them.

### Six paths, 66 skills
| Path | What it builds | Capstone (tier V) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Path of the Blade** | Melee damage, hearts, Cleave, Tough Skin, Executioner, Bloodthirst, Second Wind, Crushing Blow | **Six Light Strikes** — every sixth hit sends six slashes into every foe within 4 blocks |
| **Path of Tier Magic** | Spell power, mana, mana recovery, cheaper and faster spells, elemental focus, Overcast, Spell Surge, Mana Shield | **Maximize Magic** — every fourth spell is free and 80% stronger |
| **Path of Shadow** | Critical chance and damage, Speed, Evasion, Backstab, Venom Blade, Night Vision, Shadow Veil, Flurry | **Assassinate** — wounded foes may die outright (never bosses) |
| **Path of Faith** | Self-healing, holy spells, Smite the Undead, Purity, Guardian Aura, Divine Grace, Radiance, Turn Undead | **Halo of the Six Gods** — you and your allies keep Regeneration; holy spells +30% |
| **Path of Dominion** | Stronger, tougher and faster companions, dark spells, Soul Harvest, Legion (more summons), Despair Aura | **Authority of the Supreme Being** — companions and summons gain Strength and Speed; companions +25% |
| **Path of Fortune** | XP, Gold Coins and Data Crystals from kills, cheaper travel and forging, bigger camp and bounty rewards, Death's Bargain, Windfall | **Midas Touch** — kills drop gold, elites twice the gold |

Every path has five tiers. A tier opens once you have spent enough points in that path and reached its level
(tier II: 3 points and level 10, III: 8 and 20, IV: 14 and 35, V: 20 and 50), and some skills need an earlier
one. There are about 190 ranks in all.

### Skill points
- One at every level from level 2, and one more at every tenth level: 109 at level 100. Existing characters
  get the points for the levels they already have.
- **New item: Codex of the Supreme Beings.** Read it for one more point, up to ten. It drops from world bosses,
  the Frost Dragon, the Skeletal Dragon, the Platinum Dragon Lord (two) and Tomb Raids of tier 3 (one) and
  tier 4 (two).
- You will not have enough to learn everything: about three paths can be mastered.
- Unspent points show on the status bar as `+N SP`.

### The menu
- The front page shows each path with a progress bar and marks the paths where you can learn something now.
- In a path, every skill shows its tier, rank, and either *can learn* or why it is locked.
- Each skill's page shows what it does now, at the next rank and at full rank, with **Learn** and **Learn as
  many ranks as I can**.
- **Your bonuses** lists everything you have learned on one page.
- **Unlearn everything** gives all your points back for 10 Gold Coins plus 2 per point spent.
- Learning a skill has its own effect in the path's colour; mastering a capstone is announced to everyone.

## Install

Download `Overlord_Tomb_of_Nazarick.mcaddon` below and open it with Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.90 or newer, then
enable both packs on your world. No experimental toggles are needed.

The skill tree was checked against a mock of the game (rules, menus, combat effects, auras, kill rewards, the
Codex, unlearning and saving); it has not been played in Minecraft yet, so please report anything that feels
wrong or unbalanced.
