## v10.0.5: Familiars show their real level

- A familiar's nameplate (for example *Frost Dragonling Lv.3*) was written only when the familiar was called out.
  When it levelled up while fighting, its strength followed its new level at once, but the level shown above its
  head stayed at the old number until it was called again.
- Now the nameplate changes the moment it levels up, and is checked on every one of its steps, so it always shows
  the level it really has.
