## v10.0.0: The Everything Update

Version 10: **at least one new thing for every part of the add-on.**

| Part of the add-on | What's new |
|---|---|
| **Spells** | **Astral Smite** (tier 8, a spear of starlight) and **Black Hole** (super-tier: drags in everything within 9 blocks and crushes it) |
| **God-tier magic** | **Absolute Zero**: the cold of Cocytus's floor freezes every foe within 28 blocks solid for 8 seconds, biting each second, then shatters them |
| **Weapons** | **Ultimate Shooting Star Super**, Neia's 3D bow: shoot stars at foes, and its special attack rains a volley of five stars on foes within 24 blocks |
| **Armour** | **Pleiades Battle Maid** set (3D): Speed and Haste; tap sneak twice to throw knives at six foes and quicken your allies |
| **Creatures and bosses** | **Brain Eater**: a tentacle-faced demon caster with its own three-phase boss fight |
| **Dungeons** | **Speed records** for the Great Tomb, the Royal Capital and the Great Wall: each clear shows your time and your best |
| **Familiars** | **Wise King's Cub**, a tiny Hamsuke: Tail Whip, Forager (digs up food, gold and crystals) and Great Charge |
| **Sagas** | **Climb's saga, "The Princess's Knight"**: three tasks ending with Zero of the Six Arms; his keepsake, the **Golden Princess's Ribbon**, gives Strength and Speed when you're badly hurt |
| **Court of Nazarick** | **Sebas Tian** joins the court as **Steward** (+10% taxes, a daily Feast Pie and Black Tea) with his **Touch of Iron** move. Defeat him first to swear him in |
| **Kingdom districts** | **Grand Library of Ashurbanipal**: up to +15% spell power for its ruler |
| **Kingdom invasions** | **The Eight Fingers** (bandits, Clementine and Zero) can now attack your kingdom |
| **Campaign** | **Epilogue: The Holy Kingdom's Plea**: Brain Eater, Evil Lords Envy and Greed, then Jaldabaoth. Rewards include World Item fragments and Neia's bow, plus the title **God of Justice** |
| **Races** | **Dark Elf** (Aura and Mare's people): Speed, Night Vision, Jump Boost, and the **Forest Lord** form that roots every monster nearby |
| **Steeds** | **Hellhound Steed** (Hellfire Reins): its charge and its trampling set foes on fire |
| **Undead Army** | **Death Cavalier** can now be raised (level 35) |
| **World events** | **The Abelion Hills Uprising**: bafolk, beastmen and trolls, led by Buser |
| **Sites** | **Ruins of the Eight Greed Kings**: a ruined golden throne guarded by skeletons, liches and a Brain Eater, with a chance of a World Item fragment |
| **Arena** | A **Champions' tier** from level 80: Buser, the Quagoa Warlord, Evil Lord Envy and the Rune Golem |
| **Tomb raids** | **Sebas** and **Pandora's Actor** can now appear as raid bosses |
| **Runes** | **Rune of Shadow**: withers and weakens what you hit |
| **Gems** | **Moonstone of Tides**: restores mana on hits; in armour, Night Vision and Water Breathing |
| **Meals** | **Pestonya's Healing Soup**: Regeneration II, Resistance, and cures poison, wither and more |
| **Party** | Two new companion traits: **Lucky** (finds Data Crystals) and **Studious** (learns twice as fast) |
| **Skill tree** | **Overlord's Wisdom** (Tier Magic): more spell power, and super/god-tier spells recharge faster |
| **Titles** | God of Justice, The Princess's Ally, Swift as Death, Keeper of the Menagerie, Bane of the Hills, Heir of the Forest |
| **Creative tome** | An **Endgame kit**: divine materials, both dungeon keys and every familiar's token |

## Install

Download `Overlord_Tomb_of_Nazarick.mcaddon` below and open it with Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.90 or newer, then
enable both packs on your world. No experimental toggles are needed. Saves from older versions keep working,
including raised undead armies.

Everything new was checked in a simulation of the game's script API, and the new creatures, bow, armour,
icons, site and kingdom were drawn offline. None of it has been played in Minecraft itself yet.
