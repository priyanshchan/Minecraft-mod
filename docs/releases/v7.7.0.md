## Weapons in 3D

All 32 melee weapons and staves are now **held as 3D models**, in first and third person, instead of flat
pictures. The inventory icons are unchanged.

- **Designs:** each weapon has its own model.
  - The Staff of Ainz Ooal Gown has seven serpents holding seven gems.
  - Katanas have curved blades with glowing edges, and the Death Knight Flamberge has a rippling blade.
  - Kilineyram carries purple runes and Safarlisia a winged gold guard.
  - Ginnungagap is a great bardiche and Charon's Guidance a scythe.
  - There are rune-carved hammers, the Angel's Mace with its halo, the Frost Giant's Maul and the spiked
    Martial Lord's Club.
  - Staves are crowned with orbs, crosses and living crystal.
  - Aura's whip has a hanging lash, CZ's rifle a scope, and the gauntlets are worn on the fist.
- **Glow:** runes, edges, gems and orbs glow in the dark.
- **Elemental auras:** in third person, motes of the weapon's element drift from its tip.
- **Holds:** blades and hammers are held forward and up like a vanilla sword, staves upright, the rifle levelled.
  Every swing, art and finisher animation carries the model with your arm.

### New techniques for every weapon
- **Plunging Strike:** strike while falling to slam everything near the target. Fall harder to hit harder.
- **Charging Strike:** strike while sprinting to drive the blow through a line of foes.
- **Perfect Parry:** tap sneak just before a melee hit lands. You take no harm, the attacker is staggered and
  struck back, and a new parry animation plays.

## Install

Download `Overlord_Tomb_of_Nazarick.mcaddon` below and open it with Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.90 or newer, then
enable both packs on your world. No experimental toggles are needed.

How the weapons sit in the hand was worked out and checked with a renderer that uses the game's own bone
setup and its documented first-person arm position. The models have not been seen in Minecraft itself yet. If a
weapon looks offset or turned the wrong way in your hand, say which one and in which view, and it is a one-number fix.
