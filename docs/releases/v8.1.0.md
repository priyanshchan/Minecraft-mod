## Races, Steeds and 3D Armour

### Races of Yggdrasil
Take a race beside your class from the new **Race** page of your Adventurer's Plate. Your first race is free.
- **Leveling:** each race levels on its own from a quarter of all the XP you earn, up to race level 15.
- **Ranks:** it grows through three ranks. For example, a Skeleton becomes an Elder Lich and then an Overlord.
- **True form:** from the second rank, **hold sneak and jump** to take your true form for 30+ seconds.

| Race | Gifts | Weakness | True form |
|------|-------|----------|-----------|
| **Human** | learns faster, hits harder, regenerates when hurt | none | Heroic Spirit |
| **Undead** | immune to poison and wither, never hungry, sees in the dark, breathes water | fire | Overlord Form: an Aura of Despair |
| **Vampire** | blows drink life, strong at night | the sun | Bloodfallen |
| **Demon** | fire-proof, sets foes alight | water | Demonic Form: a ring of hellfire |
| **Angel** | glides, heals in daylight, smites undead and demons | the Nether | Seraph: heals everyone near |
| **Dragonkin** | extra hearts, tough hide | water | Dragon Form: fire breath |

- **Titles:** reaching a race's third rank earns its name as a title, such as Overlord, True Vampire,
  Arch-Devil, Seraph or Heir of the Dragon Lords.
- **Changing race:** reincarnation as another race costs 100 Gold Coins.

### Steeds
- **Nazarick Wyrm:** a young purple and gold dragon that you ride and **fly**. Use the Whistle of the Nazarick
  Wyrm to call it.
  - Jump to take off.
  - It flies where you look while you press forward, climbs while you hold jump, and hovers when you let go.
    Sprint to fly faster.
  - Use the whistle while riding to **breathe fire** at whatever you face.
- **Soul Eater Steed:** a fast undead warhorse called with the Reins of the Soul Eater.
  - It tramples monsters it runs into.
  - Use the reins while riding to **charge** through a line of foes.
- **Barding:** armour for your wyrm, your Soul Eater or a tamed Hamsuke.
  - **Adamantite Barding:** Resistance I.
  - **Barding of Nazarick:** Resistance II, Fire Resistance and Regeneration.

### Armour in 3D
All eleven armour sets are now real 3D models when you wear them:
- **Dark Warrior:** Momon's horned helm and red cape.
- **World Champion:** a plumed helm with a blue cape and tabard.
- **Overlord's Regalia:** a crown and a spiked collar on a long purple robe.
- **Other sets:** dragon horns, ram horns with glowing cracks, a holy hood, a ranger's cowl and quiver, a winged
  helm, a great helm, a dragon-skull helm with ribs and spine, and a beast-head hood with a fur collar.
- **Glow:** eye slits, gems and runes glow in the dark.
- **First person:** in first person only the sleeves show, so nothing blocks your view.

### For testing
The Creative Tome of Ascension can now raise your race to its top level, so its true form is ready at once.

## Install

Download `Overlord_Tomb_of_Nazarick.mcaddon` below and open it with Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.90 or newer, then
enable both packs on your world. No experimental toggles are needed. Saves from older versions keep working.

Races, steeds and barding were checked in a simulation of the game's script API. The armour models were drawn
offline on a player-shaped mannequin, built on the player's own bones the way the game's armour is. None of it
has been seen in Minecraft itself yet. If a piece of armour sits off your body, or the wyrm's saddle is too high
or too low, say which and it is a one-number fix.
