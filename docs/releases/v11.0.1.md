## v11.0.1: Anime companions

**This is the first download with the companions overhaul.** The v11.0.0 download by mistake contained the
previous version (v10.0.6); everything described in v11.0.0 is in this one.

### The companions redrawn in anime style
- All 32 companions are rebuilt like hand-made **Blockbench anime models**: chunky pixel-art shading, slim builds
  and long legs, smooth skin and anime faces.
- **Two face styles:** big soft eyes (with whites, a coloured iris and a shine spot) for the bright and kind, and
  narrow sharp eyes for the cool and dark ones.
- **Hair in real 3D:** separate bangs and side locks, long hair that flows down the back in loose strands and fades
  to a second colour at the tips, ponytails and twin tails that curve, braids and buns.
- **Flared, pleated skirts**, thigh-high boots and stockings, layered plate and pauldrons, capes, wizard and witch
  hats, veils and helms.
- **The Grave Maiden** (Necromancer) is now a demon of the grave: curled horns, great bat wings with bone frames,
  glowing violet eyes and black hair fading to magenta. The **Valkyrie** has feathered wings, the **Priestess** a
  halo, and the **Death Witch** small horns under her hat.

### Also in this download (from v11.0.0)
- 8 vocations with a new art every 10 levels up to 100, a path at level 30 and a mastery at 60 (232 arts).
- Companions defend you on their own, choose their arts by need, and fight as a team. New tactic: Hold fire.
- Use a companion to talk, high five, emote or give orders; 20 new animations and reactions.
