## The Great Tomb of Nazarick, and dragons that fly

### The Great Tomb of Nazarick
A five-floor dungeon deep underground:
1. **The Catacombs**: Shalltear's domain, with Old Guarders, Skeleton Mages and Vampire Brides, and spike traps.
2. **The Glacier**: Cocytus's domain, with walls of ice, a Frost Giant and freezing vents.
3. **The Amphitheater**: Aura and Mare together, with beasts and thorns.
4. **The Lava Floor**: Demiurge's domain, with Hellhounds, Shadow Demons, magma pools and flame jets.
5. **The Throne Room**: Albedo, then **Ainz Ooal Gown** himself.

- **Enter** by stepping onto the crystal inside any Mausoleum of Nazarick, or with the new **Key of the Great
  Tomb** (crafted, or found in a Mausoleum's chest). The first entrance opens the Tomb beneath that spot; every
  later one leads to the same Tomb. Players beside you come along.
- On each floor the garrison wakes when you arrive; when it has fallen, the floor's Guardian appears, with the full
  boss fight from 7.5. When the Guardian falls, the gate opens and takes the party down.
- Traps are set into the floors, and each floor holds two treasure chests.
- Conquering the Throne Room pays 120 Gold Coins, 16 Data Crystals, 6 Refining Stones and 2 gems. The first time
  it also gives two Codices of the Supreme Beings and an announcement to the server.
- Use the Key inside to climb out. A party entering an empty Tomb finds it restored for a fresh run.

### Flying dragons
The Frost Dragon, Skeletal Dragon and Platinum Dragon Lord now **take to the sky** once wounded:
- They climb with a gust, **circle** high over you and **rain breath down** while they follow you.
- Then they mark a spot on the ground, hang over it and **dive**, landing with a heavy shockwave.
- Aloft their legs fold back and their wings beat hard; only arrows and spells reach them. They fly again every
  half minute or so, more often when enraged.

## Install

Download `Overlord_Tomb_of_Nazarick.mcaddon` below and open it with Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.90 or newer, then
enable both packs on your world. No experimental toggles are needed.

The dungeon floors were built and drawn with the add-on's own preview tool, and both features were checked
against a mock of the game. They have not been played in Minecraft yet. Please report anything that misbehaves:
a dragon stuck in the air, a floor that does not open, or a gate that does not take you down.
