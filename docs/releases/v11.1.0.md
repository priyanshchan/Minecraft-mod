## v11.1.0: The world redrawn

### Every NPC redrawn
All 86 NPC models are rebuilt in the style of the anime companions. Each NPC keeps the same size it had.

- **People (45):** the Guardians and Pleiades of Nazarick, the heroes and villains of the New World, and the guards,
  soldiers, archers, knights, priests, bandits and villagers on its roads. They have anime faces, hair in 3D locks and
  their own outfits and weapons:
  - Albedo's horns, black wings and halberd
  - Shalltear's gown and lance
  - Demiurge's suit, glasses and plated tail
  - Sebas's tailcoat
  - the battle maids' armour, with CZ's eyepatch and rifle and Yuri's spiked gauntlets
  - Aura and Mare as dark elves
  - Pandora's Actor's egg face and uniform
  - Ainz's skull, crimson orb and spiked collar
- **Monsters (41):**
  - the undead: skeletons, liches, wraiths, ghouls, and the Death Knight with horns, a flamberge and a tower shield
  - goblins, ogres, trolls and giants
  - lizardmen and beast-folk, and the quagoa
  - demons with horns and bat wings, and fire-haloed angels
  - stone and rune golems, and treants
  - Hamsuke, the Soul Eater, the Hellhound, the Basilisk, the Dark Young and the Eight-Edge Assassin
  - Cocytus, and Eclair the penguin
- The maids and others in short skirts now walk on their legs.

### NPCs fight with arts of their own
Forty-five kinds of NPC now have one to three special attacks each: fireballs, lightning, raised skeletons, life drain,
terror, cleaves, ground pounds, charges, pounces, volleys, poison thorns, petrifying gazes, blinks behind you, heals,
shields and battle roars.
- Monsters use them against players and companions.
- Guards, soldiers, archers, adventurers, paladins and the heroes use them against monsters.
- Named characters call out their arts. Bosses in their fights keep their own move sets.
