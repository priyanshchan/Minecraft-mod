## v9.7.0: Divine Arms - super-tier and god-tier weapons and armour

Super-tier and god-tier spells now have gear to match: **six divine weapons** and **two divine armour sets**.

### The rule of the divine arms
- **Super-tier** arms answer only to **level 70** and up; **god-tier** arms to **level 80** and up (the same
  levels as super-tier and god-tier spells).
- Below that level they are only metal: their special attack, their on-hit power and their set bonuses do nothing.
- The **Arsenal** page on the Adventurer's Plate shows each item's tier and the level it needs.

### New materials
- **Divine Ingot:** crafted (2 at a time) from Adamantite Ingots, Royal Runes, Demon's Hearts and a Floor Guardian
  Crest.
- **Fragment of a World Item:** the god-tier material. It drops from Ainz (1-2), the Platinum Dragon Lord (2),
  Jaldabaoth (1) and Zy'tl Q'ae (1), and is awarded for conquering the Great Tomb (1), freeing the Royal Capital
  (1) and breaking the siege of the Great Wall (2).

### Super-tier armour: Seraph's Holy Plate (level 70)
White and gold plate with winged helm and tabard.
- **Made by upgrading** each Platinum piece with a Divine Ingot and two Angel Feathers.
- **2 pieces:** Regeneration I.
- **Full set:** Resistance II, Regeneration I and Fire Resistance; **sneak in the air to glide**.
- **Armour art (tap sneak twice): Seraphic Ascension** - you rise on wings of light, smiting every foe within 7
  blocks and healing yourself.

### God-tier armour: Raiment of the Supreme Beings (level 80)
Black, gold and violet, crowned with golden rays and a floating halo.
- **Made by upgrading** each Overlord's Regalia piece with two World Item fragments and a Divine Ingot.
- **2 pieces:** Strength I.
- **Full set:** Strength II, Resistance II, Regeneration I, Fire Resistance, immunity to poison and wither, **+25%
  spell power**, and **Last Stand**: once every five minutes, dropping below a fifth of your health restores all
  of it and shields you.
- **Armour art (tap sneak twice): Presence of the Supreme Being** - every foe within 12 blocks is struck,
  weakened and slowed, and you are shielded and strengthened.

All six weapons are 3D in your hand, and both sets are 3D on your body.

## Install

Download `Overlord_Tomb_of_Nazarick.mcaddon` below and open it with Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.90 or newer, then
enable both packs on your world. No experimental toggles are needed. Saves from older versions keep working.

The weapons, armour, level rule and Last Stand were checked in a simulation of the game's script API, and the
weapons, armour and icons were drawn offline. None of it has been played in Minecraft itself yet.
