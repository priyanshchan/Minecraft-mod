## v10.0.6: Healers keep you topped up

- **Healer companions mend you sooner.** A Healer retainer's Mend used to wait until you had lost two fifths of your
  health (below 60%). It now heals you as soon as you drop below **85%**, every five seconds while you need it,
  in battle or out of it. Other companions are still mended below 60%, and the most wounded is always helped first.
- **Every heal on a player goes through one place** (healPlayer): companions' Mend and Sanctuary, healing spells, the
  Cleric's regeneration and Sanctuary, race and weapon healing, the Ring of Sustenance, Last Stand and the god-tier
  heals. An add-on with a health system of its own can take these heals over, so they still count in full there.
