## Rise of the Sorcerer King

### The campaign
Ten story chapters, from a burning village to the throne. Open the **Chronicle** page of your Adventurer's
Plate and begin the next chapter. It is fought where you stand.

- **Intro:** the camera circles the battlefield while the characters speak. Sneak to skip.
- **Allies:** they join you, such as Gazef, Zaryusu, Brain and Climb, or Lakyus and Evileye.
- **Battle:** foes arrive in stages, and the last stage is the chapter's boss with its full phased fight.
- **Rewards:** each chapter pays XP, coins, crystals and items, and some give titles.
- **Failing:** dying, leaving the battlefield or running out of time fails a chapter. You can start it again at
  once.
- **Replays:** finished chapters can be played again for a quarter of the reward.

### The Sorcerer Kingdom
Found your kingdom from the Kingdom page or with the Seal.

- **Capital:** a capital of Nazarick's stone rises where you stand. Raise it through four tiers:
  - **Throne Keep:** a throne hall with a crystal throne.
  - **Walled Town:** houses, market stalls and a well.
  - **Fortress City:** a fortress wall with spires.
  - **Sorcerous Capital:** 47 x 47 blocks, with corner towers, a great spire and a lantern colonnade.
- **Outposts:** clear an enemy camp and claim it. It never posts an enemy garrison again, and it adds to your
  income.
- **Treasury:** every 20 minutes of play, taxes reach the treasury. It holds 5 days. Collect it from the menu,
  or sneak + use the Seal.
- **Guards:** Death Knights guard the capital and each outpost while you are near.
- **Invasions:** the Theocracy, demons, beastmen or quagoa and giants march on the holding you are at.
  - Beat three waves for spoils and renown.
  - If you fail, you lose an outpost, or the capital's treasury is looted.
  - Invasions can be turned off.
- **Travel:** go to any holding for free.
- **New titles:** Saviour of Carne, Dark Hero Momon, Hero of Gehenna, Equal of the Dragon Lord, Sorcerer King,
  Lord of Many Lands and Sorcerous Majesty.

### For testing
The Creative Tome of Ascension has a new **Finish the campaign** button. It crowns you at once and gives you
the Seal, so you can try the kingdom straight away.

## Install

Download `Overlord_Tomb_of_Nazarick.mcaddon` below and open it with Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.90 or newer, then
enable both packs on your world. No experimental toggles are needed. Saves from older versions keep working.

The chapters, the kingdom and its invasions were checked in a simulation of the game's script API, and the
capital's four tiers were drawn offline. None of it has been played in Minecraft itself yet. If a chapter's
foes spawn somewhere odd or the capital sits badly on your land, say where and it can be adjusted.
