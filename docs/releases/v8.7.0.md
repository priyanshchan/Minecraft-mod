## The Party Overhaul

### Detailed retainers
All twelve looks have been redrawn with full outfits, faces and gear:
- **Knight and Swordsman:** plate armour, pauldrons, a cape, a longsword and a kite shield.
- **Ranger and Hunter:** a hooded cloak, bracers, a recurve bow and a quiver of arrows.
- **Sorcerer and Wizard:** a long robe, a hood or pointed hat, a spellbook and a crystal staff.
- **Priest and Acolyte:** holy vestments, a mitre or circlet, and a staff with a halo.
- **Assassin and Thief:** leathers, a hood and mask, and twin daggers.
- **Minstrel and Skald:** fine clothes, a feathered hat or fur mantle, and a lute.

### More attacks, each with its own animation and effects
Every vocation now has **six attacks**: a new technique at level 14 and an ultimate at level 45.
- **Fighter:** Taunt, Blade Tempest.
- **Archer:** Pinning Shot, Starfall Arrow.
- **Mage:** Arcane Rift.
- **Healer:** Purify, Divine Aegis.
- **Rogue:** Shadowstep, Death Lotus.
- **Bard:** Song of Swiftness, Requiem.

Every attack plays its own animation: shield bashes, bow draws, sky shots, prayers, throws, lute strums, spins,
slams and casting gestures. A flash lights the hand that strikes.

### Smarter in battle
- **They dodge boss attacks:** members read a boss's warning and step out of slams, aside from lines, into the
  safe middle of rings, and off burning ground.
- **They fight from their place:**
  - Archers, mages and bards step away from foes that get too close.
  - Fighters throw themselves in front of foes threatening you or the back line.
  - Healers stay at your side.
- **They work together:** Pinning Shot, Shadowstep and Arcane Rift stagger foes. Every blow on a staggered foe
  hits 40% harder, and members call out the opening.
- **They pick targets well:** in Free hand they go for whatever is hurting you first, then elites, then staggered
  foes. Rogues and archers finish off the wounded.
- **They have personalities:** each member is Brave, Cautious, Cheerful or Stoic, and talks like it.

## Install

Download `Overlord_Tomb_of_Nazarick.mcaddon` below and open it with Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.90 or newer. Your
party keeps its members, levels and bonds, and each existing member is given a personality.

The party's new attacks, dodging and teamwork were checked in a simulation of the game's script API, and the new
looks were drawn offline. None of it has been seen in Minecraft itself yet.
