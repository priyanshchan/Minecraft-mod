## Creative Tome of Ascension: a testing aid

A new item for trying everything quickly. It works **only in creative mode**: get it from the creative inventory
or with `/give @s overlord:ascension_tome`. It cannot be crafted.

Use it to open a menu:
- **+1 level**, **+10 levels** or **level 100**. You get the stat points and skill points of every level passed,
  and your mana is refilled.
- **Learn every spell**, god-tier included.
- **Unlock god-tier magic**: counts the Great Tomb as conquered.
- **Full health and mana, spells ready**: also clears every spell's recharge.
- **Test kit**: Data Crystals, Gold Coins, Refining Stones, Adamantite Ingots, 10 Codices, a Key of the Great Tomb,
  Floor Guardian Crests, mana potions and 4 of each gem.
- **Back to level 1**: refunds stat points and forgets learned skills.

**Sneak + use** is a quick +10 levels. In survival or adventure mode the tome does nothing.

## Install

Download `Overlord_Tomb_of_Nazarick.mcaddon` below and open it with Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.90 or newer, then
enable both packs on your world. No experimental toggles are needed.
