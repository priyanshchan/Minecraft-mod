## v11.2.1: Grown-ups look grown up

In v11.2.0 everyone had a big head on a figure shrunk to the old height, so even the adults looked like children.

- **Adults are taller:** about an eighth taller than before and taller than the player. Their legs are a quarter
  longer, and their heads only a little bigger than a normal Minecraft head.
- **Wider builds, by character:** Gazef, the dwarf, Zero, the knights, soldiers, warriors and monks are broad; the
  curvy women are fuller; the sages, elves and mages stay lean.
- **Gowns and capes** lengthen with the legs, so Albedo's gown and the knights' capes still reach low.
- **Children stay small:** Aura, Mare, Shalltear, Entoma, Evileye, CZ, Enri and Neia keep the big head on a small
  figure.
- **Same look otherwise:** everything else from v11.2.0 is unchanged (rounded edges, faces, hair, figures and
  outfits).
