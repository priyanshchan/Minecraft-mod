## Weapons and armour reworked

### Every weapon fights in its own style
- **Kinds and elements.** All 32 melee weapons and staves now belong to a kind (blade, greatsword, heavy, dagger,
  polearm, fist, staff, whip) and an element, which colours everything they do.
- **Combos.** Land three blows in quick succession and the third is your kind's finisher:
  Crescent Cut (blades), Cleaving Arc (greatswords), Earthbreaker (heavy weapons), Thousand Cuts (daggers),
  Piercing Thrust (polearms), Rising Dragon (fists), Arcane Burst (staves) and Chain Lash (whips).
  The action bar counts the chain.
- **Awakening.** The harm you deal fills a gauge; when it is full the weapon sparks in your hand. **Sneak + use**
  to awaken it for 12 seconds: +35% harm, an elemental burst around every target, and the weapon blazes.
- **Eleven new weapon arts** for weapons that had none: Blood Lance (Spuit Lance), Azure Draw (God Slaying Emperor
  Blade), Undead Wrath (Death Knight Flamberge), Twin Blade Storm (Dark Warrior Greatsword), Fourfold Slash (Razor
  Edge), Stored Spell (Clementine's Stiletto), Dragon Fist (Sebas's Iron Gauntlets), Feral Pounce (Fang Dagger),
  Judgement (Angel's Mace), Swarm (Entoma's Blade Bug) and Dragon's Cleave (Dragonbone Greatsword).
- **Crescents** of the weapon's colour on every blow.
- **Five new attack animations:** spin, thrust, uppercut, iaido draw and the two-handed awakening.

### Armour sets
- **Two-piece bonuses** for every set (Resistance, Speed, Fire Resistance, Jump Boost, Haste, mana, a little
  lifesteal or magic protection, depending on the set). Four pieces keep the full bonuses they had.
- **Auras:** a full set wraps its wearer in its own particles: Infernal embers, Dragonscale frost shards, Dark
  Warrior and Bone Dragon wisps, holy rays on the Scripture Vestment, World Champion runes, and more.
- **Armour Arts:** with a full set, **tap sneak twice quickly**:
  Dark Charge, Dimensional Cut, Despair Aura, Frost Breath, Hellfire Nova, Sanctuary, Arrow Rain, Blood Frenzy,
  Platinum Bulwark, Bone Prison and Hunter's Howl (30–45 s recharge).

### Look
- Weapon and armour icons are now **32×32**: lit from the top right, with bright cutting edges, glinting gems and
  a faint glow in the weapon's element.
- Armour as worn is **128×64** with bevelled, shaded plates.

### Arsenal
A new **Arsenal** entry on the Adventurer's Plate shows the weapon in your hand (kind, art, finisher, gauge) and
the armour you wear (pieces, bonuses, art and its recharge).

## Install

Download `Overlord_Tomb_of_Nazarick.mcaddon` below and open it with Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.90 or newer, then
enable both packs on your world. No experimental toggles are needed.

Everything was checked against a mock of the game (combos, finishers, awakening, every new art's targeting,
armour bonuses, double-tap arts, the Arsenal page) and the new art was rendered and inspected; it has not been
played in Minecraft yet, so please report anything that feels off.
