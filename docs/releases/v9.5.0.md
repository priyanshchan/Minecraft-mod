## v9.5.0: Sagas of the New World and the Dwarven Royal Capital

A massive content drop: **sagas** (named characters you can talk to, with their own quest chains) and a second
dungeon, **the Dwarven Royal Capital**.

### Sagas: talk to the people of the New World
Six named characters now **talk with you** and each has a **saga**, a chain of three tasks:

| Who | Saga | How to talk | Keepsake (off hand) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Gazef Stronoff** | The Warrior-Captain | use (right-click / tap) | Warrior-Captain's Sigil: Strength |
| **Enri Emmot** | The Village of Carne | **sneak** + use | Charm of Carne Village: +2 hearts |
| **Nfirea Bareare** | The Bareare Apothecary | **sneak** + use | Ring of the Bareare Apothecary: mana keeps flowing |
| **Lakyus** | The Blue Rose | use | Blue Rose Brooch: Resistance |
| **Zaryusu Shasha** | The Four Tribes | use | Ring of the Four Tribes: breathe underwater, Speed in water |
| **Pinison** | The Dryad's Promise | **sneak** + use | Dryad's Blossom: heals you while you stand on grass or moss |

Enri, Nfirea and Pinison still trade when you use them normally; sneak to talk instead.

- **Conversations:** ask about the things they care about, offer a gift they like, and take up or report on
  their task.
- **Tasks** ask you to slay foes (bandits, ogres, ghouls, hellhounds...), bring something (wheat, cod, nether
  wart), find a place (a demon gate, the Dryad's Spring, the Sunlight Scripture's camp...) or defeat a great
  enemy (Nigun, Go Gin, Evil Lord Wrath, the Gigant Basilisk, Zy'tl Q'ae...).
- **Rewards:** gold, XP and items for every task; the last task of each saga gives that character's
  **keepsake**.
- **Affinity:** Stranger, Acquaintance, Friend, Sworn Ally. Tasks, questions and gifts raise it.
  - A **Friend** gives you something every day you visit.
  - A **Sworn Ally** also blesses you every day (Gazef's Strength, Lakyus's Resistance, Pinison's extra hearts...).
- **Sagas** page on the Adventurer's Plate shows every saga and how far you are.
- **New titles:** Friend of the Kingdom, Honorary General of Carne, The Sixth Rose, Hero of the New World,
  Beloved of Many.

### The Dwarven Royal Capital: a second dungeon
The lost capital of the dwarves, three floors deep under the mountains.

- **Getting in:** step onto the **rune stone** at the back of a **Besieged Mine** (newly generated mines), or
  use a **Rune Key of the Royal Capital**. Keys are found in Besieged Mine chests, sold by dwarves, and
  crafted from Royal Runes, gold and an iron block. Use the key inside to climb back out.
- **The Abandoned Mines:** a quagoa clan, then the **Quagoa Warlord** (new boss). Watch for **cave-ins**.
- **The Rune Forge:** quagoa and a frost giant, then the **Rune Golem of the Royal Forge** (new boss). Watch for
  **rune glyphs** that throw you into the air.
- **The Frozen Throne:** a hall tall enough for a dragon to fly in. Frost giants and quagoa, then
  **Olasird'arc, the Frost Dragon**, on the dwarf king's hoard. Watch for **frost** traps.
- **Rewards:** treasure chests on every floor, gold and **Royal Runes** for every floor cleared, and freeing the
  Capital gives 150 gold, runes, Data Crystals, Frost Dragon Scales and a sapphire.
- **The Runeblade of the Dwarf King** (new 3D greatsword, damage 15): given the first time you free the Capital
  (and sometimes after). Its art, **Rune Sovereign's Decree** (sneak + use), blazes three runes along the
  ground ahead (frost, storm, then fire) and wards you with Resistance.
- **New title:** Liberator of the Royal Capital.
- The Capital is never built on top of the Great Tomb, and is restored for the next party when nobody is inside.

## Install

Download `Overlord_Tomb_of_Nazarick.mcaddon` below and open it with Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.90 or newer, then
enable both packs on your world. No experimental toggles are needed. Saves from older versions keep working.

The sagas, the Capital and the Runeblade were checked in a simulation of the game's script API, and the halls,
new bosses and Runeblade were drawn offline. None of it has been played in Minecraft itself yet.
