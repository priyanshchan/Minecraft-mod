## Comforts of Nazarick

A small content drop: meals, rings, and a new undead archer.

### Meals
- **Sebas's Black Tea:** Haste II and Speed I for 90 seconds, plus 30 mana.
  - Recipe: a glass bottle, sugar and cocoa beans make two cups.
  - The Nazarick Maid sells it too.
- **Nazarick Feast Pie:** regeneration, 2 extra hearts for 5 minutes, and very filling.
  - Recipe: egg, sugar, wheat, cooked beef and an apple.
- **Spicy Dragon Skewer:** Fire Resistance for 3 minutes and Strength for 1 minute.
  - Recipe: a dragon bone, 2 cooked beef and blaze powder make two skewers.

### Rings for your off-hand
- **Ring of Sustenance:** in your off-hand it keeps you fed and slowly heals you while you stand still. You can
  still use it for a full meal once a minute, as before.
- **Ring of the Fleet Foot:** Speed and Jump Boost while worn. Craft it from a Data Crystal, gold ingots and a
  rabbit's foot.

### New enemy: the Skeleton Archer
Nazarick's lowest archer. It shoots from a distance, burns in daylight, appears at night and guards the Katze
Plains Barrows.

## Install

Download `Overlord_Tomb_of_Nazarick.mcaddon` below and open it with Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.90 or newer. Saves
keep working.

These additions were checked in a simulation of the game's script API, and the archer and icons were drawn
offline. None of it has been played in Minecraft itself yet.
