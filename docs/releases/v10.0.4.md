## v10.0.4: Data Crystals from every monster

Data Crystals, which you spend in the Grimoire to learn spells, used to come almost only from bosses, a few strong
creatures and elites. Ordinary monsters (goblins, ghouls, skeleton mages, ogres, bandits, and every vanilla
monster) never dropped any, so learning new spells was very slow.

- **Every monster you slay** now has a chance to drop a Data Crystal where it falls: one kill in ten, more against
  stronger (higher-level) monsters, up to three in ten. This counts Overlord's creatures, vanilla monsters and
  monsters from other add-ons.
- Bosses still carry their own, larger hoards; elites still give one more besides.
- Summoned allies and the arena's waves drop none, so they cannot be farmed.
- Kills by your retainers count as yours.
- The Adventurer's Plate's "How it works" page now lists where crystals come from, including the Nazarick Maid and
  the Dwarf smith, who sell them for 6 Gold Coins.
