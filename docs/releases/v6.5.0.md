## Every creature reworked

This release changes how all 83 creatures look and move. Their abilities, health and drops are unchanged.

### True to size
A grown man now stands exactly as tall as the player. Women, youths, goblins and dwarves are smaller, and the
big things are properly big: an Ogre is half again a man's height, a War Troll nearly twice, a Frost Giant
well over twice, the Primal Earth Elemental and the Dark Young about three times, and the dragons larger
still. Hit boxes follow the new sizes.

### More detail
- Every creature texture is drawn at twice the resolution, with light from above, bevelled edges, a thin
  shadow under each painted feature and a little grain.
- New geometry on the shared body types: hair that stands off the head, pauldrons, capes, belt pouches;
  tusks, ears and loincloths on ogres, trolls and giants; spines, ears and tails on beasts; back ridges and
  bracers on lizardmen and beastmen; extra horns, flank plates and a spiked tail on dragons.

### Animation
Every creature breathes, recoils when struck and leans into its stride. A fighter's off arm and head follow
each swing, capes stir at rest and stream out when running, and beasts' tails sway.

### Effects
- Something hangs in the air around the notable creatures: shadow round the undead lords, embers round
  demons, frost round creatures of ice, light round angels, arcs round lightning casters, runes round mages.
- A melee blow leaves a crescent in the attacker's colour.
- Deaths are marked: a burst, a skull over the undead, a nova for a great foe.
- A great foe arrives inside a magic circle.

## Install

Download `Overlord_Tomb_of_Nazarick.mcaddon` below and open it with Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.90 or newer, then
enable both packs on your world. No experimental toggles are needed.

Because creatures changed size, a few may briefly be stuck in tight spaces in worlds where they were already
standing. The new looks have been rendered outside the game but not yet seen inside it; if something looks
wrong, please open an issue naming the creature.
