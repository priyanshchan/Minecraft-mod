## God-tier magic

Three new spells beyond super-tier, built to be overkill:

### The Goal of All Life Is Death (dark)
- A clock of twelve marks forms round you, ten blocks across. A hand of shadow sweeps round, lighting one mark every
  half second while a bell tolls lower and lower, and dread slows and weakens every monster within 40 blocks.
- When it strikes, the world goes black for a moment and **every lesser monster within 40 blocks dies**. Bosses lose
  a third of their health.
- Skulls rise from the dead and their souls stream into you, healing you and restoring mana.
- Then **four Death Knights** rise round you to serve for a minute.

### Pantheon (holy)
- A halo opens high in the sky, pillars of light fall round its rim, and **six Archangel Flames** descend to fight for
  you.
- For ten seconds a **pillar of judgement** strikes a foe every half second, twice as hard on the undead and demons,
  while you and your allies are healed.
- At the end every foe in range is struck at once under a dome of light. You also gain Regeneration II and
  Absorption IV.

### Cataclysm (fire)
- The sky reddens over the place you point at and a 22-block circle marks it. **Thirty meteors** rain down across it.
- Then **one colossal meteor** falls on the centre: layered explosions, pillars of flame and a shockwave out to 30
  blocks.
- It deals 150 harm within 12 blocks, throws back everything within 26, and leaves the ground burning.

### For all three
- A new 3-second **invocation animation**: you kneel, then rise with arms to the sky. Three spreading magic circles,
  a pillar of light and a dome of runes go with it.
- Your **camera pulls back** to watch the spell, and returns afterwards.
- Everyone near sees the spell's name in great letters, and the whole server is told who invoked it.
- They strike **only monsters**. Villagers, animals, your companions and friendly NPCs are spared.
- **Learning:** level 80 and 48 Data Crystals, from the Grimoire, and only once you have **conquered the Great Tomb of
  Nazarick**.
- **Casting:** 180–200 mana each and a five-minute recharge. They gain mastery like any other spell.

## Install

Download `Overlord_Tomb_of_Nazarick.mcaddon` below and open it with Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.90 or newer, then
enable both packs on your world. No experimental toggles are needed.

The spells were checked against a mock of the game (timings, damage, who is spared, allies, the camera being
handed back, the learning rules); the visuals themselves have not been seen in Minecraft yet. They draw a great
many particles; on a weaker device you may see a moment of lag at the biggest moments.
