## More God-tier and Super-tier Magic

### New super-tier spells (level 70)
- **Wish Upon a Star:** you and every player near you are fully healed and shielded. All your other spells are
  ready again at once.
- **Creation:** the land where you aim freezes over 12 blocks. Every enemy on it is locked in ice for 8 seconds,
  taking cold damage each second.
- **Summon Behemoth:** two Behemoths rise from pillars of fire and fight for you for a minute, on top of your
  usual summons.

### New god-tier spells (level 80, after conquering the Great Tomb)
- **Requiem of the Forty-One:** the banners of Ainz Ooal Gown fill the sky. **Forty-one beams**, one for each
  Supreme Being, rain down on your enemies, then a final blow strikes and weakens them all. Your allies fight
  stronger under the guild's banner.
- **Ouroboros:** the world serpent coils around the battlefield. **Time turns back** for your side, so every ally
  is fully healed and cleansed. Time almost stops for your enemies, who are crushed by the coils for ten seconds.

Like the other god-tier spells, both pull the camera back for a cinematic view, light up the sky and announce
themselves to every player.

## Install

Download `Overlord_Tomb_of_Nazarick.mcaddon` below and open it with Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.90 or newer. Learn
the new spells from your Grimoire. Saves keep working.

The new spells were checked in a simulation of the game's script API. None of it has been played in Minecraft
itself yet.
