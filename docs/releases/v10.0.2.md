## v10.0.2: No more effects in your face

Weapon and spell effects no longer form inside or right in front of your character's head.

### What was wrong
- Most effects started at a "hand" point only 0.7 blocks in front of your eyes, so in first person they
  burst right across your view.
- **Weapon swings and arts:** the basic slash, several arts and the bow's star volley started at eye height,
  or drew beams that passed through your head (for example, to enemies beside or behind you).
- **Awakening:** awakening a weapon fired a pillar of light up through you, and an awakened weapon puffed
  flame in front of your face every second.
- **Pillars and bursts:** level-ups, race forms, Last Stand, the Cherub's Guardian Light and summoning a
  Guardian all shot a pillar of light through your own body.
- **Hearts and skulls:** healing hearts appeared at your head, and some skulls hovered just above it.
- **Spells:** the casting flourish, the dome of some armour arts and Wish Upon a Star's falling star also
  passed your eyes.

### What it does now
- **From your hand:** effects start at your weapon hand, at chest height, a little to the right and at least
  1.2 blocks out. In first person they appear low and to the right, where your weapon is.
- **Slashes and bursts:** these appear 2 or more blocks ahead of you at chest height.
- **Beams:** a beam to an enemy beside or behind you now leaves on that side, so it never crosses your head.
- **Pillars on you:** rings at your feet, light rises around you, and the column starts above your head.
- **Awakening:** a ring and a wide spiral around you; the awakened glow stays at your waist.
- **Hearts and skulls:** hearts float above your head; skulls loom ahead of you, over your enemies.
- **Projectiles:** the Staff of Ainz's fireball, the rifle's bullet and spell projectiles leave from the
  hand, not the eyes.
- **Arrow Rain:** it never drops an arrow through you.

### Checked
A new automatic test uses every weapon's art and own ability, lands blows, awakens a weapon, uses every
armour art and casts every spell short of god-tier. It fails if any effect forms within one block of the
player's eyes.

## Install

Download `Overlord_Tomb_of_Nazarick.mcaddon` below and open it with Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.90 or newer, then
enable both packs on your world. No experimental toggles are needed. Saves from older versions keep working.

This was checked in a simulation of the game's script API, not in Minecraft itself.
