## The Court of Nazarick and a Living Kingdom

### The Floor Guardians serve you
Every Floor Guardian you have defeated can now be **sworn to your Sorcerer Kingdom**. Open **Court of
Nazarick** on the Kingdom page.

- **At court:** each Guardian holds a post that shapes your realm, and stands in your throne hall while you are
  home.
  - **Albedo**, Overseer: taxes +25%, and a bigger treasury.
  - **Demiurge**, Strategist: smaller invasions, bigger spoils, less looting, and no raided caravans.
  - **Cocytus**, General: more guards, and stronger ones.
  - **Shalltear**, Vanguard: joins the fight in every invasion.
  - **Aura**, Beastmaster: outposts pay more.
  - **Mare**, Druid: citizens settle twice as fast.
  - **Pandora's Actor**, Treasurer: richer trade, and more wares.
- **At your side:** bring one Guardian along as a companion with a signature move:
  - Albedo's Wall of Iron
  - Demiurge's Hellfire Wall
  - Cocytus's Frozen Heaven
  - Shalltear's Purification Lance
  - Aura's Breath of Fear
  - Mare's Earth Surge
  - Pandora's Actor's Doppelganger, which borrows another Guardian's move

  Their court post stands empty while they're with you.
- **Loyalty:** it grows as you fight together and repel invasions, and it makes their moves stronger.
- **Falling:** a fallen Guardian recovers in Nazarick for five minutes.

### A living kingdom
- **Eight districts** outside your capital's walls, each built and improved to level 3:
  - **Market:** taxes, and trade routes.
  - **Dwarven Smithy:** Refining Stones.
  - **Alchemist's Workshop:** mana potions.
  - **Barracks:** guards.
  - **Magic Academy:** XP on everything.
  - **Temple of the Supreme Beings:** Data Crystals.
  - **Farms** and **Houses:** room for citizens.
- **Citizens** settle every kingdom day, pay taxes, and walk your streets as villagers.
- **Trade routes:** caravans run from your Market to your outposts and pay every day, unless bandits raid them.
- **Envoys:** the Re-Estize Kingdom, the Baharuth Empire, the Slane Theocracy, the Dwarves, the Lizardmen and
  the Holy Kingdom send envoys to your throne.
  - **Ally** with them for daily gifts, **demand tribute** for wealth now, or **dismiss** them.
  - Make an enemy of the Theocracy and it marches on you more often. Make it an ally and it never does.
- **New titles:** Master of the Guardians, Beloved Sovereign and Merchant King.

### For testing
The Creative Tome of Ascension can count every Floor Guardian as defeated, so they can all be sworn in at once.

## Install

Download `Overlord_Tomb_of_Nazarick.mcaddon` below and open it with Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.90 or newer, then
enable both packs on your world. No experimental toggles are needed. Saves and kingdoms from older versions
keep working.

The court, districts, trade and envoys were checked in a simulation of the game's script API. The capital with
all eight districts was drawn offline. None of it has been played in Minecraft itself yet.
