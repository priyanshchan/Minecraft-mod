## v11.2.0: The new anime look

### Every companion and person redrawn
All 32 companions and the 45 people of the world (Albedo, the Pleiades, Clementine, Lakyus, the guards and the rest)
are redrawn in a new anime style, modelled on popular anime Minecraft models.

- **Bigger heads**, on figures that still stand as tall as before.
- **Big eyes** set low under heavy lashes, with sparkle highlights, a small mouth and soft blush.
- **Fuller hair**, with thick locks framing the face.
- **Twice as fine textures**, with smooth shading down the whole figure.
- **Rounded edges everywhere**: the head, hair and hats, arms and hands, legs and feet, the body and the clothes.

### Figures and fashion
- Grown women have an hourglass figure (fuller chest, narrow waist, curvier hips and thighs) and longer legs.
- Men have broad shoulders tapering to a narrow waist.
- Over-knee socks with bare thighs under short skirts.
- Cropped tops on Clementine, the Huntress, Shieldmaiden, Nightblade, Lotus Fist and Blade Dancer.
- Bare shoulders on Albedo, the vampire brides, the Songstress, Witch, Death Witch, Enchantress, Grave Maiden and Wind
  Disciple.
- Chokers, small hair clips (crossed pins, bars, bows or stars), bigger sleeves and stronger lip colour.
- Aura, Mare, Shalltear, Entoma, Evileye, CZ, Enri and Neia keep their outfits and a plain figure.

### Fixes
- Beards hang below the mouth instead of covering it: a neat beard for Sebas, stubble for Gazef, and full or long
  beards for the dwarf, Fluder and the Wizard.
- Hair stays inside open helmets instead of poking through.
- Albedo's wings fold down from the small of her back.
- The archer's and Minstrel's hat feather is a slender plume.
- Beast-folk and quagoa fur is flecked, not striped.
- Gargantua's runes are carved glyphs.
- Evileye has a proper mask.

Monsters keep their shapes and sizes, painted twice as finely.
