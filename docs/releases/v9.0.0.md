## v9.0.0: The Army of the Dead and the World Aflame

A mega content drop: **necromancy** and **world events**.

### Necromancy: raise your own Undead Army
- Everything that falls near you leaves a **corpse** for 30 seconds, with souls drifting over it.
- Use the new **Book of the Dead** near corpses to **raise them**:
  - **Skeletons** from weak bodies.
  - **Elder Liches** from stronger ones.
  - **Soul Eaters** from stronger still.
  - **Death Knights** from the mightiest, from level 45.
- Your undead **serve for good**:
  - They follow you and fight whatever threatens you.
  - They **rank up every 5 kills** (up to five stars), growing hardier and stronger.
- **Army size** grows with your level, up to 16.
- **Undead race:** an Undead player commands more of them, raises them for half the mana, and raises them
  stronger.
- **Commanding them:** sneak + use the Book, or open the new **Undead Army** page, to give orders (follow, hold
  your ground, gather to me), rename them, or release them.

### World Events
Every half hour or so, a world event is proclaimed to every player. It appears somewhere out in the world, with
directions and a beacon to find it.
- **The Battle of Katze Plains:** the undead march out of the fog, with the Empire's knights fighting beside
  you. A **Skeletal Dragon** leads them.
- **The Theocracy's Crusade:** priests and angels, ending with the **Dominion Authority**.
- **The Demon Plague:** hellhounds and demons pour out of a rift, and **Jaldabaoth** follows.
- **The Dragon Descent:** quagoa and Frost Giants, then **two Frost Dragons**.

Reach the event to begin it, fight through its waves and its boss, and score for every foe you, your party or
your undead slay.
- **Rewards:** everyone who fought earns XP, Gold Coins and **Medals of Valor** by score. The best fighter earns
  more.
- **Medal shop:** on the new **World Events** page, medals buy Codices, Guardian Crests, gems, Refining Stones,
  the Book of the Dead, the Barding of Nazarick and even the Wyrm Whistle.

### New titles
Necromancer, Lord of the Dead, Hero of Katze, Crusade Breaker, Plaguebane and Dragonwatch.

### For testing
The Creative Tome of Ascension can start a world event near you straight away.

## Install

Download `Overlord_Tomb_of_Nazarick.mcaddon` below and open it with Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.90 or newer. Saves
keep working.

The army and the events were checked in a simulation of the game's script API, from raising and ranking the dead
to a whole event from proclamation to victory. None of it has been played in Minecraft itself yet.
