## v11.0.0: The Companions Overhaul

The party system is rebuilt from the ground up.

### 32 new high-detail characters, men and women
- Every companion is drawn anew at four times the texture detail: real eyes with irises, lashes and a glint, brows,
  blush, beards, and hair in strands. Hair styles include short, long, bob, ponytail, twin tails, braid, buns, topknot
  and wild manes.
- **Women for every vocation:** Valkyrie, Shieldmaiden, Huntress, Windrunner (elven), Witch, Enchantress (elven),
  Priestess, Battle Sister, Shadow Dancer, Nightblade, Songstress and Blade Dancer.
- **Two new vocations:** the **Monk** (Martial Monk, Ascetic, Wind Disciple, Lotus Fist) fights with fists, feet and
  ki. The **Necromancer** (Necromancer, Bone Sage, Death Witch, Grave Maiden) raises the dead.
- Each class has its own gear: plate with pauldrons and capes, winged and horned helms, hoods, wizard and witch hats,
  veils, circlets and crowns. Weapons include swords, a greatsword, a katana, a spear, axes, a mace, bows, staffs
  topped with orbs, crystals, haloes or skulls, a scythe, daggers, curved blades, lutes, shields, books and quivers.
- The twelve old looks keep their numbers, so your companions keep their vocation; they simply look far better.

### A new art every 10 levels, up to 100, with branching paths
- Three core arts per vocation (levels 1, 10, 20).
- **Level 30:** choose one of two paths (arts at 30, 40, 50).
- **Level 60:** choose one of the path's two masteries (arts at 60, 70, 80, 90 and a capstone at 100).
- 8 vocations, 16 paths and 32 masteries make **232 arts** in all. Every one of the old 36 arts is still there.
- Retrain a path for 25 Gold Coins. Mercenaries hired at level 30 or more arrive with a path already chosen.
- Summoning arts call wolves, skeletons, ghouls, Death Knights or a Behemoth. These do not use up your own summon
  slots and crumble when their time is up.

### Far better AI, in both mods
- **Companions defend you on their own:** any monster near you is fought without waiting for your first blow. This
  works on Overlord, vanilla and other add-ons' monsters, including RSCraft's. Zombie pigmen are left alone unless
  they start it, and companions do not run up to creepers (their arts still strike them from where they stand).
- **Smart choice of arts:** heal the hurt first, shield the party before a boss's blow, sweep crowds, save the
  capstone for elites. Companions mix their arts instead of repeating one.
- **Teamwork:** members focus the same foe, avenge a hurt companion, flankers slip behind your target, and the
  front line steps between you and danger.
- **New tactic, Hold fire:** the member starts no fights and only answers attacks on you or the party.

### Interactions and animations
- **Use a companion** to talk (+1 bond a day), high five, emote, give orders (follow, wait, go ahead), gift it, or
  open its full page. **Sneak + use** gives a quick high five.
- **Emotes:** wave, bow, cheer, dance, laugh, salute, sit, think. The party menu has an emote for everyone at once.
- **20 new animations:** punches, kicks, a scythe's reap, summoning, channelling, a whistle, a dodge roll, flinching
  when hit, and victory poses.
- **Reactions:** companions wave when they arrive, cheer when they learn an art or level up, celebrate a won fight,
  share a thought on a quiet road, sit down at night or while you sleep, and call out their great arts by name.
