## v10.0.1: Weapon placement fix

### Every weapon is now held the same way
- Before, each kind of weapon had its own angle in the hand, so they didn't line up with each other:
  - **staffs** (and Neia's bow) stood almost upright;
  - **daggers** leaned further forward;
  - **hammers and clubs** leaned a little less.
- Now every weapon you hold uses the same pose, in first person and in third person.
- **Exceptions:**
  - The **Magic Rifle** is still aimed straight ahead.
  - **Gauntlets** are still worn on the fist.

### Longinus fixed (and renamed)
The god-tier spear from v9.7 shared its name with the older **Longinus** World Item that Rubedo drops, and
the older one replaced it. As a result the spear:
- was drawn at a different angle from every other weapon;
- did no weapon damage;
- used itself up (and three of your levels) instead of doing **Erasure**.

It now has its own name, **Longinus Reforged, the Spear That Erases**, and works as described in v9.7. Craft it
as before: a Spuit Lance, four World Item fragments and two Divine Ingots. Rubedo's one-shot **Longinus** is
unchanged.

### Under the hood
The add-on's own checker now catches any name defined twice, and any 3D weapon that isn't held like a weapon,
so this can't happen again.

## Install

Download `Overlord_Tomb_of_Nazarick.mcaddon` below and open it with Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.90 or newer, then
enable both packs on your world. No experimental toggles are needed. Saves from older versions keep working.

The new hold pose was checked by drawing every weapon in first and third person offline; it has not been
checked in Minecraft itself yet.
