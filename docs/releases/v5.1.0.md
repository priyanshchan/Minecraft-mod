## Camps, outposts and villages now actually appear

In earlier versions the faction sites (camps, outposts, Carne Village, the Mausoleum of Nazarick and the rest)
almost never showed up in a world. This release fixes that.

**What was wrong.** The game only lets an add-on read or place blocks in chunks that are loaded and ticking —
with a low simulation distance, little more than 60 blocks around you — and raises an error for anything
further away. The old code tried to build a site while you were 40 to 110 blocks from it, treated that error
as a failure that also stopped every other region from being checked, and then deliberately refused to build
once you were closer than 40 blocks.

**What changed.**

- A site is built as soon as you come within about 70 blocks and its chunks are ready; if they are not, it is
  simply tried again two seconds later.
- One region failing no longer stops the others from being looked at.
- Sites are more common: about two in three 160-block regions hold one, so there is usually one within a
  couple of hundred blocks, and they may now be as close as 64 blocks to the world spawn.
- Uneven ground is evened out with a dirt foundation instead of being rejected, and a spot that is under
  water or too steep moves up to 24 blocks to one side.
- More kinds of ground count as buildable (terracotta, deepslate, snow, ice and other stone), and tree tops
  and snow cover are looked through properly.
- The **Travel** entry of the Adventurer's Plate menu now names the nearest site nobody has found yet, with
  its distance and direction.
- Worlds played with an older version are surveyed again from scratch, so sites appear there as well.

## Install

Download `Overlord_Tomb_of_Nazarick.mcaddon` below and open it with Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.90 or newer, then
enable both packs on your world. No experimental toggles are needed. If you already had an older version,
remove it from the world first and add this one.

The add-on's logic is checked by an automated simulation, but this build has not been play-tested in the game
itself. If sites still do not appear, please open an issue.
