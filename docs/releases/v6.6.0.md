## The great dragons

The Frost Dragon, the Skeletal Dragon and the Platinum Dragon Lord have been rebuilt from nothing. Their
health and drops are unchanged; everything else about them is new.

### Size
They are now by far the largest creatures in the add-on: about 23, 24 and 30 blocks from snout to tail, with
wingspans of about 20, 22 and 27 blocks. A man reaches roughly to a dragon's knee.

### Three different dragons
- **Frost Dragon:** pale blue scales, tall ice spines, a beard of icicles and a mace of ice on its tail.
- **Skeletal Dragon:** an open ribcage round a green soul fire, a bare skull and tattered wings.
- **Platinum Dragon Lord:** plated flanks, a crown of golden horns, a glowing gem in its chest and a bladed
  tail.

They share one skeleton — a two-part neck, a hinged jaw, four clawed legs, a four-part tail and two-part
wings — so all three move the same way.

### Animation
Wings beat in two parts and harder on the move, the neck weaves, the jaw gapes to bite and to snarl, a wave
runs down the tail, and a bite is a lunge of the whole neck.

### Effects and attacks
- **Roar** at the first player a dragon sees, shaking the view of everyone within 48 blocks.
- **Presence:** each is wreathed in its own element along its wings, tail and body.
- **Footfalls** shake the ground and raise dust.
- **Breath:** a circle marks where it will land while light gathers in the jaws; a second later it strikes
  that spot, so it can be dodged. Frost Breath slows and leaves the ground freezing, the Breath of Undeath
  withers, and the Platinum Dragon Lord's Judgment is five bolts of lightning.
- **Wing buffet:** a gale that hurts and throws back everyone standing close.
- **Enrage** at half health: Speed, Strength, and faster breath and buffets.
- **Death:** pillars of its element along its whole length, then a dome of magic circles.

## Install

Download `Overlord_Tomb_of_Nazarick.mcaddon` below and open it with Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.90 or newer, then
enable both packs on your world. No experimental toggles are needed.

The dragons stay on the ground; they do not fly. Their hit boxes are their trunks, not their whole length, so
blows aimed at the neck or tail can pass through. The new models were rendered outside the game but have not
yet been seen inside it; if a dragon looks or behaves wrongly, please open an issue.
