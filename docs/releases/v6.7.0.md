## Spells that light the world, and stronger companions

### Spell lighting and effects
- **Light.** Spells now light up their surroundings: the casting hand, the ground under a magic circle, the
  length of a beam, the point of impact, pillars from base to top and novas out to their edge. This is real
  block light, so it shows in every graphics mode; with Vibrant Visuals it falls on the scene the way a
  torch's light does. It is white rather than coloured.
- **What a spell leaves behind.** Flames burn on and the ground is scorched where fire struck; mist hangs
  where cold, shadow or acid struck; lightning goes on crackling over a scorch mark; light and runes keep
  rising after holy and arcane magic.
- Beams have a soft glow round their core, and motes circle a caster of the fifth tier and above.
- Seven new particle effects (32 in all).
- **Grimoire → Spell lighting** switches the lighting off for the world if you would rather not have it.

### Companions
- **A set of attacks each.** Every vocation now has four attacks (the Mage five), learned at levels 1, 8, 20
  and 35 and used in turn, about one a second. New: Cleave and Earthshatter (Fighter); Fire Arrow and Rain of
  Arrows (Archer); Chain Lightning, Frost Nova and Meteor (Mage); Smite and Blessing (Healer); Poison Flask,
  Throwing Knives and Shadow Flurry (Rogue); Dissonance and Crescendo (Bard).
- **Stronger.** Base health is 60 (was 40), they gain two hearts every 5 levels (was every 8) and Strength
  every 20 (was every 30), melee damage and firing rates are up, and attack damage is roughly half as high
  again.
- Heals and the Rallying Cry are always used first when they are needed.

## Install

Download `Overlord_Tomb_of_Nazarick.mcaddon` below and open it with Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.90 or newer, then
enable both packs on your world. No experimental toggles are needed.

Spell lighting works by setting an invisible light block for a fraction of a second and removing it again.
Only air is ever replaced, and any light left behind by a crash is removed when the world next loads. This
has been checked in simulation but not yet seen in the game; if a spot stays lit, please open an issue.
