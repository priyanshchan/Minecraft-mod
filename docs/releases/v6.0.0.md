## The arena, runes and World Items

A large content release: twelve new creatures, four new sites, weapon runes, an arena to fight in and three
World Items for the end of the game.

### New creatures
- **Bosses:** Rubedo (the strongest being in Nazarick), the Platinum Dragon Lord, Decem Hougan the Elf King
  and Go Gin the Martial Lord.
- **Foes:** the Primal Earth Elemental, Eight-Edge Assassins that arrive invisible, and bands of Bafolk
  Raiders.
- **Allies and neutrals:** Remedios Custodio, Neia Baraja and the Holy Kingdom's Paladins; Elf Rangers; and
  the Arena Master.

### New sites
Imperial Arena, Holy Kingdom Fort, Elven Grove (all friendly, and reachable through Travel once found) and the
hostile Bafolk Raid Camp.

### Runes
A Dwarf Runesmith can inscribe one rune on a weapon: Fire (burns), Frost (slows), Storm (a second jolt of
lightning) or Life (heals you for 8% of the damage dealt). Runes are crafted from Data Crystals or bought
from the runesmith, and are written on the weapon beside any reinforcement.

### Imperial Arena
Speak to the Arena Master to fight five rounds for Gold Coins and XP. The champions follow your level; from
level 50 the last two rounds are Zero of the Six Arms and Go Gin, the Martial Lord.

### World Items
- **Avarice and Generosity** hoards a tenth of every kill's XP while you carry it.
- **Depiction of Nature and Society** seals away every lesser monster around you.
- **Longinus** erases any one foe, at the price of three of your levels.

### Also new
Martial Lord's Club, Staff of the Elf King (summons a Primal Earth Elemental), Platinum armour, sigils for
Decem, the Platinum Dragon Lord and Rubedo, seven guild requests (56 in all), the Martial Lord title, and the
new bosses in the world-boss rotation.

## Install

Download `Overlord_Tomb_of_Nazarick.mcaddon` below and open it with Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.90 or newer, then
enable both packs on your world. No experimental toggles are needed.

The add-on's logic is checked by an automated simulation, but this build has not been play-tested in the game
itself. If something does not work, please open an issue describing what you did and what you saw.
