## Structures overhauled, and seven new sites

### Every site rebuilt
All 21 existing camps, forts and villages have been redesigned as proper settlements — larger (up to
27 blocks across), with real buildings instead of a few blocks round a campfire:

- **Houses** with floors, doorways, glass windows, a lantern inside and a pitched roof that overhangs the walls.
- **Towers** with battlements, arrow slits and a lantern on top; **forts** with curtain walls, corner towers
  and a gate; log **palisades**; rings of stakes; round walls.
- **Pavilions** for commanders, tents that open towards the middle of the camp, market stalls, a covered well,
  trees with lookout platforms, paths, plazas and lamp posts.
- Highlights: Carne Village is a fenced village with four roads, three cottages, a smithy, a well and a bell;
  Kingdom Army Camps have a palisade, a command pavilion, a watchtower and a training yard; Empire Outposts and
  Holy Kingdom Forts are stone forts with four corner towers (the Fort adds barracks and a quartz chapel); the
  Mausoleum of Nazarick has a pillared portico; the Elven Grove is six great trees with lookouts; the Dwarven
  Forge has a blast furnace, smithing table and a smoking chimney; the Demon Gate is eight blocks high.
- Each site is laid out the same way every time from its position, keeps its middle clear for its garrison,
  and several now hold two chests.

### New sites
| Site | Garrison |
| --- | --- |
| **Roadside Inn** — inn with bar, kitchen and tables, a stable, a merchant's stall (friendly, can be travelled to) | Adventurers and Kingdom Guards |
| **Mage Tower** — 16-high tower with three floors, bookshelves, a mana font, scaffolding up to the roof | Skeleton Mages; Fluder Paradyne from level 20 |
| **Ruined Keep** — crumbling walls, one standing tower, a roofless hall with cobwebs, two chests | Old Guarders; Vampire Brides; Death Knights and a Soul Eater |
| **Frost Giant Hold** — round wall of packed ice crowned with blue-ice fangs | 1–3 Frost Giants |
| **Besieged Mine** — a shaft with pit props into a stone hill, ore and spoil heaps, the miners' overrun camp | 4–8 Quagoa |
| **Angel Sanctum** — quartz colonnades lit with glowstone, a high altar before a window of gold | Archangel Flames; a Dominion Authority from level 50 |
| **Den of the Wise King** — a mossy burrow under four trees | Hamsuke |

28 kinds of site in all. The hostile new sites can be cleared for Gold Coins and XP like the others.

### Existing worlds
Every site an earlier version built is rebuilt on the same spot in its new form the next time you come near
it (its chests get fresh loot) and stays discovered. Regions the earlier version left empty stay empty, so
nothing is dropped onto ground you may have built on; the new kinds of site appear in land you have not
explored yet.

### For contributors
`python3 tools/preview_sites.py` builds every site against a mock world, draws it, and reports anything standing
where the garrison is posted or reaching outside the site. The tests now check the same for every site.

## Install

Download `Overlord_Tomb_of_Nazarick.mcaddon` below and open it with Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.90 or newer, then
enable both packs on your world. No experimental toggles are needed.

The new layouts were checked by building them against a mock of the game and drawing them; they have not
been walked through in Minecraft yet, so please report anything that looks wrong.
