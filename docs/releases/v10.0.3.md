## v10.0.3: Soulbound tools, and a multiplayer check

### Your tools stay with you
- The **Adventurer's Plate**, the **Grimoire** and the **Magic Wand** (the staff you cast with) are now
  **soulbound**: when you die they are not dropped, and they are still in your inventory when you respawn.
- This works wherever you carry them: hotbar, inventory or off hand.
- New copies are bound as soon as they are handed out. Ones you already have, craft or pick up are bound within two
  seconds.
- The mark is put on the inventory slot itself, so the item in your hand is not swapped out and back.
- Everything else still drops as usual (unless the world has *keep inventory* on).

### Multiplayer check
The whole add-on was checked for multiplayer.
- Nothing assumes there is only one player.
- Every command targets only the player it is meant for.
- Camera moves and screen effects only reach the right players.
- Per player: profiles, levels, mana, quests, kingdoms, familiars, retainers, arena fights and raids.
- Shared, and built for co-op: the Great Tomb, the Dwarven Royal Capital and the Great Wall are one run per world
  for everyone who enters together, and every player on the last floor is rewarded. World events score each player.
