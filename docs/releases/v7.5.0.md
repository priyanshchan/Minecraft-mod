## Boss fights and gear upgrades

### Every boss reworked
All 18 bosses (Ainz, the Floor Guardians, Pandora's Actor, Gargantua, Rubedo, Jaldabaoth, Evil Lord Wrath,
Dark Young, Decem, Zesshi, the Dominion Authority, Go Gin and Zero) now fight in **three phases**, with
**telegraphed moves you can dodge**:
- Before each move, its name appears on screen and glowing circles or lines mark where it will land; a second or
  two later it hits whoever is still there. Slams, rings that are safe close in, single, fanned and crossed
  lines, barrages under every player, pulls and blinks.
- At two-thirds and one-third health the boss changes phase: a title on screen, a blast that throws you back,
  allies for some (Death Knights, Vampire Brides, Shadow Demons, Archangel Flames...), and new moves.
  The last phase is **enraged** and its moves come faster.
- Some moves leave **burning, frozen or poisoned ground** for a few seconds.
- 82 named moves in all, such as Reality Slash, The Goal of All Life Is Death, Walls of Jericho, Absolute Zero,
  Gehenna Flame and World's End.
- **Rewards:** everyone who fought gets 2–4 Refining Stones and a gem (two from the strongest bosses), and the
  **first** time you defeat each boss, a Codex of the Supreme Beings.
- The three dragons keep their own staged fights from 6.6.

### Gear upgrades
New **Workbench** page on the Adventurer's Plate (needs a Dwarf Runesmith, an anvil or a smithing table near):
- **Refining:** Common → Fine → Superior → Epic → Legendary → Divine, with Refining Stones and Gold Coins.
  Weapons gain 4% melee damage per step; armour pieces turn aside 1.5% of harm per step (30% for a full Divine
  set). Higher steps can fail; the stones are lost, never the item.
- **Gem sockets:** weapons open a socket at Fine and a second at Epic; armour opens one at Superior. Six new gems
  (Ruby, Sapphire, Topaz, Jade, Onyx, Sunstone), each with a weapon effect and an armour effect. Gems can be
  taken out again for 10 Gold Coins.
- **Salvage:** break a piece into Refining Stones, Data Crystals and its gems, with a chance of a bonus gem.
- Quality, reinforcement, rune and sockets are all written on the item.
- **New items:** Refining Stone and six gems, each craftable (see the README), dropped by bosses and elites, and
  sold by the Dwarf Runesmith (Refining Stones).

## Install

Download `Overlord_Tomb_of_Nazarick.mcaddon` below and open it with Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.90 or newer, then
enable both packs on your world. No experimental toggles are needed.

The boss fights and gear system were checked against a mock of the game (warnings, dodging, phases, hazards,
rewards, refining, sockets, salvage); they have not been played in Minecraft yet, so please report any move that
feels unfair or any boss that feels too easy.
