## The Magic Wand in 3D

The **Magic Wand**, the staff you cast your spells with, is now held as a 3D model in first and third person,
like the add-on's other weapons:
- a dark wooden shaft bound in gold
- a gold claw closed round a glowing Data Crystal
- a purple gem at the collar
- arcane motes drifting from its tip

Nothing else changes. Use it to cast your selected spell, and sneak + use it to switch spells. Its inventory icon is
the same.

## Install

Download `Overlord_Tomb_of_Nazarick.mcaddon` below and open it with Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.90 or newer. If the
old version is still applied to your world, replace it with this one. Saves keep working.
